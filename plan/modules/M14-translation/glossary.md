## Style
- Address the reader as “you”; Gorica writes in the first person (D18).
- No gendered forms are needed in English; Serbian „/la” forms become plain “you”.
- Dates in English texts: 30 June 2026, 14 February (day month year, no ordinals).
- Degrees: 28° Aquarius; houses in running text spelled out (“the fourth house”).
- Keep Gorica's quotes of Jung and Maslow in their standard English wording; Jung for About: “When an inner situation is not made conscious, it happens outside, as fate.” (her choice, `docs/sadrzaj/05`).
