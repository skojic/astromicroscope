# Product

Source: `docs/brief-klijenta.md` (client brief) and `README.md`. Where this file and the brief disagree, the brief wins.

## Goal
Astromicroscope becomes a recognised **education brand** for astrology. The site presents Gorica's way of working, her knowledge and her educational programs. Individual analyses stay as the exclusive, premium part of the offer.

## Who it is for
1. **Learners**: people who want to understand astrology properly. They are the main audience.
2. **Clients**: people who want a personal analysis and like Gorica's approach.
3. **Searchers**: people who arrive from Google, AI answers, YouTube or Instagram with a specific question (a transit, a sign, a term).

## Offer, in priority order
1. Educational programs (live)
2. Recorded courses
3. Digital products (guides, workbooks, yearly transit guide)
4. Original articles / blog
5. Premium one-to-one work: natal analysis, solar analysis, karmic analysis, consultations for specific areas of life

## What success looks like
- Visitors find a course or a free lesson within one screen of landing.
- Pages rank, and are cited in AI answers, for Serbian-language astrology questions. English is secondary.
- Core Web Vitals are "good" for 75% of visits, on mobile and on desktop (`specs/07`).
- No serious accessibility barriers: WCAG 2.2 AA (`specs/08`).

## Brand
- Modern, elegant, professional and high quality. Simple and practical.
- Mostly white and soft beige; blue from light to deep (Uranus: originality, innovation); small gold details.
- Design direction: **D, Midnight Lens**, chosen by Gorica (decision D08). The brand kit is `brand/`; see `specs/02`.
