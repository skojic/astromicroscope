# Visual review checklist

At 360, 768, 1024 and 1440 px, in Serbian and English, in Chrome, Safari and Firefox.

- [ ] Matches the chosen direction (`specs/02`): colours, type, spacing
- [ ] Serbian text fits: longer words do not overflow buttons, headings or cards
- [ ] č, ć, š, ž and đ render in every font and weight
- [ ] Images sharp on high-density screens; no distortion
- [ ] Hover, focus and active states present
- [ ] Empty and error states look intended
- [ ] Dark sections (for example the hero) keep the focus ring and text readable
- [ ] Print view of articles is readable (no navigation, full text)
