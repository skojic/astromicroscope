My work covers natal, predictive and karmic astrology.

## How it began

My name is **Gorica Vićentijević**. I became interested in astrology in 2000, when I became a mother for the first time. I have always been curious, and I experienced parenthood as my most important mission, something I wanted to approach with full responsibility, consciously and with dedication.

Like many parents, I met plenty of advice and opinions from those who “knew better”. Still, I decided that my real handbook would be a book a pediatrician gave me, and the planets. Learning about their influence on us and on our children, I discovered how precious it is to know a child at a deeper, unique level. Just as the Moon in the natal chart speaks of the mother and of emotional development, our understanding of that symbol shapes the foundation for relationships and growth over time.

In 2003 I became a mother for the second time. My second child was born with the Moon in Aquarius, and it was as if the universe itself whispered to me: “Mother astrologer.” That message turned me even more firmly towards my path.

## Education

Two decades later, in 2025, I graduated from the **Johannes Kepler Astrological Institute**. Today I work as a **professional astrologer**. Over years of research I have been especially drawn to **evolutionary** and **karmic astrology**, but what inspires me most is **children's astrology**. If we do not guide a child at the start of its path, we often face healing childhood wounds later on, which I now see in my work with many clients.

## What interests me most

In my astrological work I am especially interested in family inheritance, genetic predispositions, unconscious patterns, and how early childhood shapes personality and later life choices.

A particular area of my interest is the child's path of development, the different stages of growing up, and the role of parents in recognizing and supporting a child's individual potential.

## Today

Astrology has been a precious ally in raising and understanding my children, and in my own development. It helped me awaken my own potential and talents, recognize important life cycles, learn from challenges and grow through experience.

Today, after more than 20 years of work and research, I am here to pass my knowledge and insights on to you. To help you get to know yourself, your child and the relationships that shape you. To discover your inner map together: **your path, purpose and the mission of your soul**.
