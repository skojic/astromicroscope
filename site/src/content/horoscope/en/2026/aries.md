## Why is 2026 a turning point for Aries?

Ahead of you is a year that changes the ground you stand on. Two slow planets, Saturn and Neptune, enter your sign and set off a deep process of personal change. At the same time, Uranus changes sign and activates your third house, while Jupiter moves into your area of love, creativity and joy. Transits lined up like this are extremely rare, and they lead you into a completely new phase of life.
A big chapter is closing, and ahead of you is a period in which you build a new version of yourself: more mature, more stable and more authentic.

## The main themes of the year

### Saturn and Neptune in Aries: creating a new identity

#### Neptune in Aries from 26 January

Neptune's entry into your sign starts a period in which much will seem unclear or unfinished. The start of the year comes with low energy and a need to withdraw. January and February may be marked by tiredness, sensitivity and inner dilemmas. This is a phase that slows you down to make room for something new. Neptune heightens intuition, but it also creates foggy situations and blurred boundaries, so it is important to listen to your body and pay attention to your emotional reactions.
This is a period of regeneration, cleansing and release from a burden you have carried for a long time.

#### Saturn in Aries from 14 February

Saturn's entry changes the dynamic. After a period of inner haziness, Saturn brings back focus, realism and the need for discipline. From mid-February you enter a phase in which you take responsibility for your decisions, your body, your habits and your professional direction. Saturn in the first house brings clear boundaries, stability and the need to restructure your life from the ground up.
For many Aries this is the start of a new life cycle, a period in which the foundations are laid for the next seven years.

## Love and relationships

### Jupiter in Leo brings joy and emotional growth from 30 June

Jupiter's entry into your fifth house opens one of the most beautiful chapters of the year. From July you can expect more warmth, emotional openness and a need to enjoy life. Many Aries may enter stable relationships, take a significant step forward in an existing one, move in together or plan a family. This is a time when faith in love returns, and relationships develop spontaneously and naturally.

### Venus retrograde brings back people from the past (3 October – 14 November)

In this period Venus sets off important processes of re-examination. Its retrograde through your seventh and eighth houses brings back into focus themes from the past, unresolved relationships, emotional dilemmas and karmic partnerships. This is not the time for rash decisions, but it is the time to see clearly what you do and do not want in a partnership. Many Aries will meet people from the past again in this period, either emotionally or while settling legal and partnership matters.

## Work and career

### The start of the year brings a slower pace and harder conditions

January and February may bring delays, waiting and finishing obligations left over from the previous period. Many Aries will feel a lack of support, or business will move more slowly than usual. Still, this is only a transition; from March things begin to stabilize and your working energy grows stronger.

### Saturn's influence brings a fresh start or a whole new profession

As Saturn enters your sign, it activates the need to set your professional goals more seriously. This is a year in which many Aries start new projects, change direction or finally move into a more responsible and stable career.
Saturn asks for commitment, but it rewards everyone who shows perseverance and a willingness to build for the long term.

### August is the time for big agreements and professional progress

August is one of the most favorable months of the year for important agreements, collaborations and advancement. Many will sign important contracts, start new ventures or get an offer that changes the course of their career.

### Mercury retrograde and its challenges

During Mercury's retrograde periods you need to pay closer attention to documents, deadlines and communication. The periods from 26 February to 20 March, from 29 June to 24 July and from 24 October to 13 November may bring mistakes, delays and technical problems. Be especially careful with paperwork and when signing anything.

## Home and family

### Jupiter in the fourth house brings stability and a bigger home

The second part of the year brings favorable circumstances for home and family relationships. Jupiter in the fourth house can bring buying or renovating a property, moving to a bigger place, a growing family and more support from older family members. May and July are especially important months, when many Aries settle housing questions or set big family plans in motion.

## Uranus in Gemini brings new ideas, knowledge and a change of surroundings

With Uranus entering Gemini on 26 April, a period of mental freshness, new ideas and learning opens up. Your third house becomes active, which brings quick thinking, a wish to explore and the discovery of new interests. Moves, a change of surroundings or new people entering your life are possible.
Uranus sets off the need to open up to different approaches and pick up new skills that will later become key to your growth.

## Health

The start of the year brings lower immunity and a tendency toward viral infections, tiredness and exhaustion, especially under Neptune's influence. It is important to watch your diet, avoid excess and bring in physical activity gradually. As the year goes on, especially from spring, your health stabilizes and your energy returns to a higher level.

## A year that changes you from the ground up

Ahead of you is a year that brings together responsibility, transformation and wider horizons. Saturn and Neptune in your sign call you to authenticity and stability, while Jupiter and Uranus bring joy, creativity, love and mental freshness.
By the end of the year you will have more strength, clearer intentions and the readiness to step into the life you are building from a completely new position, with maturity and full strength.
