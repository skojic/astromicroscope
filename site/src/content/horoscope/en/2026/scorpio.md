## The energy of the year

Over the past years Scorpios have become much more serious. Saturn brought them less pleasure and more responsibility, life lessons and deep inner change. Even if not much changed on the outside, their inner world went through a powerful process of maturing and transformation.

2026 brings a turning point. This is a period in which you stop putting up with what drains you and start choosing yourself. The need to break free from everything that suffocates you becomes stronger than ever, and the courage to change comes from within.

## Work and career

### A turning point and professional shifts

From **14 February 2026** work and your work environment may become a source of serious strain. Greater demands, a sense of too much responsibility and constant pressure may bring you to a moment in which you question your whole professional path so far.

This year many Scorpios will decide to leave a job, change profession or start a private business. This is a year of stepping out of frames that limit you and into a space of personal freedom and professional independence.

The year calls you to action, but not to impulsive moves. Every decision should be considered, realistic and set up so that it brings you stability in the long run.

## Mercury retrograde: periods of caution

In 2026 there are three periods in which Mercury retrograde will affect you especially strongly, calling for extra care in communication, relationships and paperwork.

In the period from **26 February to 20 March** emotional themes from the past may come to the surface. Old stories, former relationships and unresolved situations ask to be cleared up. This is a time of inner insight and emotional reckoning.

From **29 June to 24 July** contacts abroad, former collaborators or people who have long been out of your life come back. If you travel, pay special attention to documents, contracts and agreements. Do not make decisions in a hurry, and avoid moves without thorough checks.

In the period from **24 October to 13 November** you enter a phase of deep re-examination. This is not the time to make important decisions in the heat of the moment. You need to slow down, see the bigger picture and be careful in traffic and when signing documents.

During the period from **3 October to 14 November** people with suspicious offers may appear. Do not trust promises without a real basis, and avoid risky deals.

## The closing message of the year

2026 gives you the chance to step out of everything that wears you down, break the patterns that block you and make room for a new version of yourself. It is a year of personal strength, inner release and bold decisions that change your life in the long run.
