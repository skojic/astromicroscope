## The year at a glance

Over the past two and a half years Libras have gone through serious challenges at work and with their health. Many faced exhaustion, heavy pressure and a loss of motivation, but 2026 brings a huge turn, especially in relationships.

From mid-February relationships with other people become the central theme of the year. Every kind of bond goes through a reality test, so it becomes clear who is in your life for sincere reasons and who is there out of habit, gain or fear.

## Love, marriage and partnership

### A year of truth and revelation

The year ahead brings emotional honesty, even when it is not easy. In 2026 many Libras will see their partner as they really are. For some this may bring an emotional cooling, while others go through personal crises that inevitably affect the relationship.

Relationships without healthy foundations may break, while stable ones will grow stronger. One thing is certain: only what is real and built on truth remains.

## Work and career

### Professional opportunities and big steps forward

During the year Libras will travel more, both for business and for pleasure. From July doors open to advancement, work in a larger team or confirmation of your professional status.

The second half of the year brings stability, serious agreements and the chance to grow a private business. Everything built earlier now pays off through praise, recognition and concrete financial gain.

### Mercury retrograde: periods of caution

Three periods during the year call for special caution in communication, work and money.

During the period from **26 February to 20 March** you need to be very careful with work obligations and paperwork. Signing contracts, making promises and closing deals all need extra checking, because administrative mistakes and legal complications are possible.

From **29 June to 24 July** the focus is on relationships with authorities and institutions. Old problems may reopen, but this time you have the chance to finally resolve them.

In the period from **24 October to 13 November** your personal and material values come up for review. Avoid risky moves with money and impulses that could lead you in the wrong direction.

## Changing where you live

This year many Libras are thinking about moving. Moves, renovations or a change of surroundings now have a strong effect on your inner state and give you back a sense of balance.

## Money

From the beginning of October debts may be settled and money may come in through insurance, court proceedings or earlier investments. During the same period, avoid risky financial decisions and investments without a clear assessment.

## Health

During October and the first half of November tiredness, low energy and lower immunity may appear. Your body is sending you a clear message that it is time for a slower pace, more sleep and building your strength through good food and rest.

## Single Libras

Changes in your love life begin as early as mid-February, and from spring many single Libras enter very romantic and unusual stories. Charismatic people from other backgrounds may come into your life through travel, studies or business contacts.

## Advice for Libra this year

2026 teaches you to choose relationships in which you have peace, to set clear boundaries and to recognize your own worth. This is a year of big decisions, but also of enormous relief. When you let go of what holds you back, the way opens to a more stable and fulfilled life.
