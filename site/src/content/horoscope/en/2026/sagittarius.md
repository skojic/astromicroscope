## The energy of the year

For Sagittarius, 2026 brings an important question: "Am I living the life I truly want?" The start of the year is marked by inner re-examination and uncertainty about your personal direction, your social standing, your emotional relationships and your business partnerships. Many will feel they want change but will not yet have a clear picture of which way they want to go.

The first three months of the year bring a slower pace and deep reflection, but they are extremely favorable for finishing everything started in previous years. 2026 does not begin with an explosion, but with a quiet release from the burdens of the past. Everything you close now prepares the ground for a new phase of life that arrives from summer on.

Finances at the start of the year may be unstable or strained, but the situation gradually improves. Money comes through work with foreign companies, joint ventures or through a partner. What matters most is to handle money thoughtfully at the start of the year, without risks or hasty decisions.

## Work and personal growth

From July your energy turns strongly toward going abroad, education, travel and widening your horizons. Chances appear to work with people from abroad, move abroad, continue your education or work with international companies. Those who are studying can achieve significant success, and those who are only planning to start their studies have strong support to do it with ease.

This year gives you the chance to earn through what you love and truly enjoy. The way you make money changes, and work becomes more personal, more meaningful and more in tune with who you are inside.

## Love, relationships and family

In 2026 emotional relationships go through an honesty test. You can no longer stay in relationships that do not fulfill you, help you grow or make you happy. Many Sagittarians will withdraw, enjoy their own company more or decide to step away from relationships that have become a burden.

For some, marriages and partnerships may reach turning points. Relationships built on unstable foundations enter a phase of serious re-examination, while stable ones have the chance to deepen and grow stronger. Saturn, which moved through your fourth house in previous years, shook family relationships and brought emotional distance, responsibilities and a sense of heaviness. Many had to mature through family circumstances and learn where their emotional boundaries are.

At the same time, Saturn in the fifth house in 2026 brings a serious view of love. Some will want to settle down and enter a stable relationship, while others will end relationships that have lost their meaning. Relationships with older or more mature people are possible, as is greater responsibility toward children.

### Retrograde periods

Mercury retrograde at the start of the year stirs up family themes, unresolved relationships and conversations you have long put off. The period until mid-March is ideal for giving time to family relationships and solving old problems.

In summer, Mercury puts the emphasis on money. Then you need to avoid risky investments, loans and unverified deals. The autumn retrograde brings low energy, a sense of exhaustion and possible disappointment through work or people in authority. Some may decide to change jobs or redefine their professional direction.

Venus retrograde in the second half of the year moves through your twelfth house and may bring back emotional stories from the past, secret relationships or feelings that were never fully cleared up. In that period you need extra caution and clear boundaries, in love and in business relationships alike.

## Health

During the year, occasional dips in energy may point to a need to withdraw and recover. The end of the year calls for extra attention to your health, because tiredness, a lack of strength and a need for emotional distance may appear then.

## Advice for Sagittarius this year

2026 teaches you to admit the truth to yourself, especially in partnerships. Do not choose to stay out of fear and habit, but out of authenticity. In the years to come, while Uranus moves through your seventh house, many relationships will go through an endurance test. This is not a period of loss, but of liberation.

Put your energy into knowledge, travel and personal growth. Money comes when you dare to do what you love. Do not chase security at any cost, because your real security comes when you live in harmony with yourself.
