The start of 2026 may bring a sense of heightened activity and scattered energy. Many Virgos will be pulled in several directions at once, spending energy and taking on too many obligations, which can lead to exhaustion, worry and mental tiredness. The most important thing in the first months of the year is to set clear boundaries with others and with yourself, to shift your focus from "I have to do everything" to "I choose what truly matters", and to learn to tell apart what drains your strength from what builds you up.

## Advancement and a big change at work

Virgos who work as part of a team can expect a promotion, a change of position or greater responsibility as early as the first part of the year, while those in private business have the chance to start working with serious organizations, companies or foreign partners. Many will rethink their professional path and decide to go independent or change careers completely. If you are in a job that no longer fulfills you, this year gives you the chance to see that clearly and take concrete steps in line with your own truth.

## Calm after a big test

For the last two and a half years, while Saturn moved through your seventh house, relationships were tested, and many got a clear picture of their own worth. In 2026 the circle that remains around you is made up of people who are honest, steady and loyal. Single Virgos have a good chance of starting a stable relationship, though there may be an age difference.

Mercury, your ruler, is especially significant in its retrograde phases, because it can bring back former partners, unfinished conversations and legal matters. These phases are not the time for new beginnings, but for finishing what has been started and sorting out earlier situations.

## Mercury retrograde

From 26 February to 20 March, Mercury in your seventh house may bring meetings with people from the past and reopen old topics.

From 29 June to 24 July, while it moves through your eleventh house, old friendships and business contacts may be renewed and earlier plans completed.

From 24 October to 13 November, Mercury in your third house is a reminder to take care in communication, in traffic and when signing documents. This is a period for wrapping up old matters, but not for making important new decisions.

## Health, abroad and money

The start of the year may bring lower immunity, a tendency toward respiratory infections and tiredness, so it is important to pay attention to hormones, gland function and the nervous system. In June there is a greater risk of injury, so take care with physical activity and avoid wearing yourself out.

From October to mid-November chances open to renew contacts abroad, continue your education, take courses or work on yourself. This is a year of strengthening your inner authority and the need to live by your own values, not other people's expectations.

Finances call for caution throughout the year, and investments and long-distance travel need extra attention. These situations call for thoughtfulness and a realistic approach.

## Advice for Virgo this year

2026 teaches you to choose yourself, save your energy, build relationships on truth and let go of everything that does not support your growth. This is a year of maturity, empowerment and healthy decisions. Through focus, discipline and inner peace you reach outer stability and prepare the ground for a life true to yourself.
