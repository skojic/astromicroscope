For Cancer, 2026 brings a period of rebuilding peace, stability and a sense of solid ground under your feet.
Under the strong influence of Jupiter and Saturn you enter a cycle of maturing, consolidation and laying foundations for a long period ahead. Chances, opportunities and rewards come to those who have worked hard, while those who avoided responsibility may now feel the consequences. This is a year that shows clearly how much investing in yourself has paid off.

## The main themes of the year

Until **30 June 2026** Jupiter stays in your first house, bringing optimism, luck, ease, support and a wave of new opportunities. You will stand out more and attract people, and many wishes may come true faster than usual. This is a period of personal growth and expansion.

When Jupiter moves into Leo on **30 June**, doors open to increase your personal and material worth through new business opportunities, work with foreign companies, promotions or better-paid positions.

**From 14 February** Saturn enters your career sector and puts a clear question in front of you: **What have you done so far, and how hard have you tried?**

With persistence and diligence, Saturn brings you stability, rewards and higher status.

If obligations have been neglected, things may get harder, with greater responsibilities or even a change of job.

Uranus moves through your 12th house and crosses the Moon, bringing inner release, a wish for change and even a move. This is the energy of breaking old emotional patterns and giving birth to a new inner strength.

The start of the year may be hard: worries, limits, fears and mental tiredness may weigh on you. Still, if you approach each challenge responsibly, you will feel the rewards later.

## Love and partnership

Emotional relationships may be demanding and weighed down by responsibility. Many Cancers will feel pressure, but also the need to give their partner more attention, see the relationship more realistically and set healthy boundaries.

Single Cancers may meet interesting people through work, their workplace or colleagues.

In the second part of the year emotional disappointments are possible, not because of your mistake, but because the Universe is showing you who truly belongs in your life.

Venus retrograde, from **3 October to 14 November**, brings back people from the past: former loves, and those who never quite forgot you. Secret admirers may also appear.

## Career and money

This is one of the best years for laying solid foundations in your career. Cancers who work on themselves, learn and make an effort will see fantastic results. Finances stabilize, and from July chances for more serious growth open up.

## Challenging periods

- **26 February – 20 March:** old contacts from abroad may return. If you travel far, keep your documents safe.

- **29 June – 24 July:** a period of uncertainty and mistakes. Do not make big decisions.

- **24 October – 13 November:** someone from the past or a secret admirer gets in touch, but act wisely.

The year ahead brings stability, progress and the chance to finally build a secure structure for your life. The more bravely and responsibly you approach challenges, the greater the rewards will be. This is a year of maturing, growing stronger and consolidating.
