Since 2018, when Uranus entered Taurus, briefly went back into Aries and then re-entered Taurus in 2019, people born under this sign have been in a long process of inner and outer change. Uranus has set many Taurus natives in motion, moved them out of their comfort zone and confronted them with the need to be more independent, more honest with themselves and ready for life-changing change.
They felt it most in their emotional relationships, where many faced the truth and saw their own needs more clearly.

## Money, work and career

In 2026 Uranus moves through their house of personal and material values. This brings new ways of earning, a change of business direction or collaborations that offer more freedom. Many Taurus natives may change jobs, modernize the way they work, start their own business or bring in new methods that give a steadier income.

Saturn in the 12th house marks a period of withdrawal, introspection and preparation for a new life cycle. This is a time to face subconscious fears, finish old stories and close emotional chapters. Saturn asks for inner discipline and putting things in order, so that 2027 can bring a great release.

### Education, travel and ideas

In the first part of the year Jupiter in the 3rd house brings chances to learn, take extra courses, build skills and expand the mind. Many short trips and lively communication are also possible. This is a period when new ideas come spontaneously and open new paths.

## Love and relationships

The year brings important reckonings in emotional relationships.
Some Taurus natives may experience disappointment, a wish for distance or a clearer understanding of their emotional needs.

Venus retrograde (**3 October – 14 November**) moves through their 7th and 6th houses, so old emotional stories, contacts from the past or relationships that need closure may come back to life.

## Mercury retrograde

- **26 February – 20 March:** old contacts get in touch, a feeling of being stuck.
- **29 June – 24 July:** busy with short trips; take care in traffic and communication.
- **24 October – 13 November:** Mercury through the 7th house brings old partnership themes back to life, and possibly legal matters from the past.

## Home, family and property

From **30 June**, when Jupiter enters Leo and activates the 4th house, a strong period opens for Taurus to settle the housing question.
Many will buy or renovate a property, enlarge their home or grow their family. This is one of the most favorable periods in years for investing in property.

For Taurus, 2026 is a key year for work, finances and living space.
It is a year of big but useful changes, with a strong need to build a stable base for the next cycle of life.
Education, investing in knowledge and the courage to take up new opportunities will be their greatest allies.
