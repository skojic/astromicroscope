## The energy of the year

For Aquarius, 2026 is one of the more significant years. As a sign that naturally seeks change, freedom and novelty, Aquarians will feel a strong push of fate this year. Their rulers, Saturn and Uranus, change sign, and with that the direction of their energy changes too.

Saturn, which until now has been testing your system of personal and material values, enters the sector of communication, decisions and mental stability on 14 February. This brings a period of serious conversations, important paperwork, new studies and new business directions. From February many Aquarians make final decisions they have long put off. This phase may feel tiring because it calls for responsibility, discipline and facing reality, but in the long run it brings stability.

Uranus enters the sector of love, creativity and the joy of life and awakens dormant emotions. Your love life becomes stormy, intense and unpredictable. Sudden new relationships and unexpected infatuations are possible, but so are quick breakups of relationships without stable foundations. Flirting on social media, unusual loves and contact with people who bring excitement will mark this year.

## Work and public life

The first half of the year brings more business opportunities, interesting contacts and a wider circle of collaborators. From summer doors open to people from abroad, international business and partnerships with potential for growth. 2026 is extremely favorable for signing contracts, business alliances and joint projects.

The seventh house, which becomes active, also speaks of formal relationships, so many Aquarians may enter marriage, public relationships or business partnerships that change their status. This is also a year of stepping into the public eye, whether through a personal brand, presenting ideas or taking on a more visible role in a team.

## Love and relationships

During 2026 emotional relationships go through a phase of re-examination. Partnership comes into focus, but so do suspicion toward a partner, different expectations and changes in the dynamic of the relationship. For some Aquarians this is a year of redefining a marriage, and for others the start of a serious relationship.

Venus retrograde in autumn further reopens old emotional themes, unsettled relationships and questions about family decisions. Dilemmas about moving, relationships with relatives and renewed emotional encounters with people from the past are possible.

### Mercury retrograde

At the start of the year the emphasis is on money, values and work, when misjudgments and unclear agreements are possible. This is not a period for risky investments or sudden business moves.

Summer brings a slowdown at work and a need for extra caution when signing contracts. Autumn opens the theme of reputation, standing and relationships with people in authority, so it is important to choose your words and not enter conflicts that could leave lasting consequences.

## Health

Mental pressure is high and your nervous system is weakened. It is important to pay attention to your diet and to exhaustion from too many obligations. From February you need more rest, good sleep and a sensible use of your energy.

## Advice for Aquarius this year

Although it comes naturally to you to react quickly and impulsively, 2026 asks you for a strategic approach. Every bigger decision calls for a plan, patience and a realistic assessment. Change is unavoidable, but it is important that you steer it, not that it steers you.
