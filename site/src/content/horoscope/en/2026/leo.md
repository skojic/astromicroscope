## Why is 2026 special for Leo?

Ahead of you is a very exciting year. **Jupiter**, the great benefic, enters your sign on **30 June 2026**, bringing luck, prosperity, growth and advancement. This is a period when new opportunities open up in love, career and personal development.

Those who work in sport or creative fields can expect significant success and recognition.
The year also brings important lessons about responsibility and decision-making, so it is the right moment to recognize what you want to achieve and what you want to build on.

## Work and career

### The start of the year: focus and care

At the start of 2026 many Leos are focused on work. Although conditions may be harder and stress greater, everything will work out in your favor if you approach your tasks responsibly.

From **14 February** changes in your team or a move to a new work environment are possible. Saturn and Mercury underline the need for care when signing contracts and making important decisions.

**Periods when you need to be especially careful with money:**

- 26 February – 20 March
- 29 June – 24 July
- 24 October – 13 November

In these periods misunderstandings, misjudgments or bad investments are possible. Avoid risky investments and important financial decisions.

### Venus retrograde

From **3 October to 14 November** uncertainty and indecision may appear, both at work and with money. People from the past may come back into your life, and it is important not to make impulsive decisions. Focus on re-examining and planning.

Jupiter in your sign brings new professional opportunities, business growth and greater influence. This is a period when you step out of the shadows and take the place that belongs to you, with recognition and advancement.

## Love and relationships

From **July** a very interesting period opens in love, especially in **June and July**.
Jupiter in your sign brings joy, emotional growth and new chances for love.

Single Leos may enter fateful relationships.

Those who are married feel a need for more freedom, which can sometimes bring changes or adjustments in the partnership.

**Venus retrograde (3 October – 14 November)** stirs up themes from the past. This is a period of introspective re-examination of relationships and of making clear decisions about what you want in a partnership.

## Home and family

The area of home and family is especially active through deep transformations in family relationships and inner life, with an emphasis on breaking free of old patterns and setting clear boundaries.

**Pluto in Aquarius** brings unexpected situations and chances for liberation. This may be a period of changes in the family, moves or renovating your home, but at its core it helps you build a more stable and authentic living space.

These energies encourage you toward:

- an introspective look at family patterns
- stronger emotional independence
- relationships strengthened by honesty

If relationships with family members have been tense until now, this is a period when deep, lasting shifts can happen, often through unexpected circumstances.

## Health

The start of the year is sensitive:

- pay attention to your bones, teeth and circulation
- look after your immunity and energy
- rest and gradual physical activity are important

As the year goes on, your energy and vitality return, especially from midsummer.

## Advice for Leo

2026 is a year of fateful change. The start may be challenging, but the middle and end of the year bring luck, new opportunities and personal expansion.
Watch your finances, re-examine your decisions and open up to new experiences in love, work and your surroundings. Jupiter and Uranus help you build a life that is truly yours: stable, authentic and fulfilled.
