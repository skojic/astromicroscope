## The energy of the year

The last two and a half years have been extremely demanding for Pisces. Saturn moved through your sign and asked you to mature, to let go of illusions, to set boundaries and take responsibility for your life. In that period many Pisces lost their old image of themselves, but gained a new one: more realistic, more mature and stronger.

On 14 February 2026 Saturn moves into your second house, and the focus shifts to security, work, money and personal values. This year teaches you how to build financial stability and a sense of security from within, without relying on others. Fears about money may come up, but they are exactly what pushes you to organize your life more seriously and plan for the long term.

## Work and money

The first half of the year calls for care with contracts, agreements and documents. Until April it is important to watch what you sign and what obligations you take on, because legal delays, misunderstandings or outside pressure are possible.

From July the situation changes significantly. Jupiter brings big business chances, growth, progress and entry into larger organizations, teams or collaborations with people from abroad. Then Pisces can achieve financial growth, more work and steadier income. Many will change their team, work environment or professional direction.

Saturn in the second house teaches you saving, managing money well and separating what you really need from what is habit and impulse. This is a year in which you lay the foundations of future security.

## Love and emotions

Until July Jupiter stays in your sector of love and brings lovely emotional opportunities, especially the chance to enter a stable, serious relationship. In this period Pisces are magnetically attractive and may meet someone they want something lasting with.

The second half of the year brings your work and love life together. Relationships that start through work, friendship or shared projects can grow into something much deeper. Many Pisces will see that security in love comes with truth, maturity and stability, not through idealization.

Venus retrograde, from 3 October to 14 November, may bring back old love stories, former partners or emotional themes that were never resolved. This is not the time for big decisions in love, but a time for inner understanding.

## Home and a change of surroundings

On 26 April Uranus enters your sector of home and family and brings big changes. Moves, renovations, changes in family relationships or sudden decisions about where you live can completely change your daily life. Many Pisces will quite literally change their life from the roots, enter new surroundings and begin a new chapter.

### Mercury retrograde

From 26 February to 20 March Mercury retrograde moves through Pisces and calls for particular care in communication, signing documents and making important decisions. This is a period of introspection, but not of impulsive moves.

From 29 June to 24 July old emotional stories and contacts from the past may come back to life.

In the period from 24 October to 13 November the emphasis is on contact with people abroad, travel and paperwork, with a need for extra attention and responsible planning.

## Health

There are no major health warnings for 2026, but attention to your bones, teeth, spine and possible inflammation is recommended.

## Neptune in Aries

Neptune's entry into Aries changes your inner experience of the world from the ground up. Pisces wake up and become more decisive, more direct and more aware of their needs. You no longer agree to silence and self-denial. You begin to express yourself clearly, to fight for what belongs to you and to step out of "invisibility".

2026 teaches you to value yourself, to stop living for other people's needs and to live in your own truth instead. Financial maturity, emotional stability and inner strength become your new foundation. And most important of all, you begin to build a life that suits you.
