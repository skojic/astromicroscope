*For Libra by Sun sign and rising sign*

Dear Libra,

Ahead of you is a year that can bring significant changes in how you see yourself, your relationships with other people, your professional opportunities and your plans for the future. You do not enter 2027 completely free of the events of the previous period.

Already in the autumn of 2026 many Libras went through an intense phase of re-examining personal values, relationships, finances and decisions made earlier. Venus, your ruler, moved retrograde through Scorpio and Libra in October and November 2026, that is, through your second and first houses.

This transit may have raised many questions that concern not only love, but also your relationship with yourself. You may have started to see certain people differently.

You may have realized that you invested too much time and energy in relationships in which you did not receive enough in return. For some Libras financial decisions, their own needs or their sense of self-worth may have come up for review. That is why the start of 2027 may be a continuation of a process that began the previous autumn. Already from 7 January, when Venus moves into Sagittarius, your third house, a different energy can be felt.

After a period of deep re-examination, a need opens up for communication, movement, learning, socializing and making new contacts. Many Libras could become more optimistic, more open to conversation and readier to think about the future again.

2027 has a much deeper symbolism. Throughout the year Saturn and Neptune stay in Aries, your seventh house, which is connected with marriage, serious partnerships, business collaborations and relationships with other people.

Their stay in this house can be one of the more important phases of maturing when it comes to relationships. Saturn asks for responsibility, clarity, boundaries and a willingness to see things as they really are. Neptune, on the other hand, can bring idealization, unclear expectations, doubts or a feeling that you do not fully understand the other person's intentions.

That is why in 2027 you may think much more seriously about whom you trust, with whom you want to continue a certain relationship and how satisfied you are with your emotional and business partnerships. Some Libras could feel a need to distance themselves from people with whom they no longer share the same values. Others may want to strengthen, define or take an existing relationship to a more serious level. Saturn in the seventh house does not necessarily mean breakups. It speaks of a need for relationships to gain clearer structure, responsibility and stability. What is good enough can become more serious. And what has long been kept alive on vague promises may call for a different approach. At the same time, in the first half of the year Jupiter moves through Leo, your eleventh house. This is the area of friendships, groups, joint projects, your social circle and long-term plans. Jupiter is retrograde at the start of the year, so many Libras may return to earlier ideas, contacts and plans that were not carried out in 2026.

It is especially interesting that Jupiter rules your third house of communication, ways of thinking, learning and everyday contacts.

That is why in the first months conversations with people from the past, earlier business agreements or plans postponed for some reason may reopen. On 13 April Jupiter turns direct again, which can help you see the future more clearly. And on 26 July it moves into Virgo, your twelfth house.

This opens a different stage of the year, in which you may feel a greater need for peace, withdrawal, finishing certain obligations and preparing for new cycles of life. That is why 2027 for Libra cannot be seen only as a period of new opportunities.

This is a year in which you will learn to tell apart relationships that support you from those that drain you, plans that have a future from those you keep out of habit, and your own needs from other people's expectations.
