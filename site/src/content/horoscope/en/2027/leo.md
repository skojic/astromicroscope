*For Leo by Sun sign and rising sign*

Dear Leo,

Ahead of you is a year that can bring significant opportunities for personal growth, professional progress and financial strengthening, but also important changes in how you see yourself and your relationships with other people.

Jupiter, the planet astrology connects with growth, prosperity, optimism and favorable opportunities, has been in your sign for some time and will stay there until 26 July 2027. This is one of the more significant transits for Leo, because Jupiter in your first house symbolizes widening opportunities, stronger self-confidence and personal ambition, and the need to achieve more than you have so far.

You may have noticed already in the previous year that you think differently about your future, that you have bigger plans or that you are no longer satisfied with circumstances that were once enough for you.

Many Leos could want to improve their life, change the way they work, start their own business, get more education or simply begin to invest more in themselves. Jupiter does not only bring opportunities. It also magnifies our expectations, wishes and ambitions, and sometimes our tendency to overdo things.

That is why in the first half of the year you will have to learn to tell apart what you can truly achieve from what you would like to achieve without enough preparation. It is especially interesting that Jupiter begins the year moving retrograde and only turns direct again on 13 April.

This means the first months of 2027 may be more about re-examining earlier decisions, unfinished plans and personal expectations.

You may realize that you do not want to continue certain things the same way. You may return to an idea you postponed earlier, or reconsider an opportunity that has already come your way.

From the second half of April circumstances may gradually become more favorable for concrete steps, new initiatives and achieving personal ambitions. The start of the year carries another important piece of symbolism. Mars, the planet of activity, initiative and struggle, is in your second house, which is connected with money, earnings, expenses and a sense of material security. Its retrograde from 10 January to 1 April can raise questions of finances, earlier investments and the way you handle money.

From 21 February Mars moves back, retrograde, into your sign, where it stays until mid-May. This period can be extremely dynamic, but also demanding. You will feel a need to get things moving, change something, make a decision or break out of circumstances that limit you. Not everything will depend only on your will.

Sometimes more patience will be needed than you are used to showing.

That is why one of the most important messages of this year is that you do not have to overcome every obstacle by force, nor seize every opportunity right away.

Ahead of you is a year in which you will learn to direct your energy where it can truly bring long-term results.

## Work and career

Work can be very significant in 2027, especially for Leos who have been thinking for a while about a professional change, advancement or starting something of their own.

Until the end of July Jupiter in your first house encourages ambition, self-confidence and the need to be noticed. Many Leos could get the chance to stand out, show their abilities, take on a more responsible position or start a business they have been planning for some time.

The start of the year calls for caution. Venus, as ruler of your tenth house of career and social standing, is in Scorpio, your fourth house, at the beginning of January.

That is why some Leos may be focused on balancing family and work obligations. You may think about working from home, changing your workspace or organizing daily life differently. Tenser aspects between Jupiter and Venus at the start of the year can heighten big expectations, especially at work. You may want more than current circumstances allow, or judge that a certain business offer carries bigger opportunities than it later turns out to. That is why it is important not to make decisions based only on promises.

From 3 February to 1 March, when Venus moves through your sixth house, attention turns to your work environment, colleagues, daily obligations and business contacts. This can be a favorable period for improving relationships with colleagues, getting recognition for earlier effort or creating a more pleasant atmosphere at work.

For some Leos opportunities for extra work or collaborations that arise through everyday contacts may open up.

From 1 to 26 March Venus moves through your seventh house.

This period can be interesting for business partnerships, negotiations, contracts and working with clients.

Since Mars and Mercury are still retrograde in early March, certain agreements may need further adjustment.

Do not rush to conclusions if a certain collaboration is not progressing as you expected.

An especially favorable period for professional growth opens from 14 May to 8 June, when Venus moves through Taurus, your tenth house.

Venus is in its domicile there, which further emphasizes the themes of professional satisfaction, social standing, earnings and business success.

In this period many Leos could get recognition, improve a business collaboration, make an important contact or present themselves to the public in a different way. For those in private business, this can be an interesting period for promotion, presenting new services or improving their business image.

From 8 June to 3 July Venus moves through your eleventh house.

Business opportunities may then come through friends, acquaintances, social media, groups and people who share your interests. An especially interesting development comes in the second half of the year.

With Jupiter entering Virgo, your second house, on 26 July, the focus gradually shifts from personal ambitions to concrete results, earnings and material security.

What you started, developed or planned in the first half of the year can now gain its financial dimension.

For some Leos this may be a period of higher income, better business offers or developing additional sources of earnings. Jupiter does not guarantee financial success on its own.

It brings opportunities that call for responsible money management and a realistic assessment of business circumstances.

The end of November and December further emphasize business activity.

On 25 November Mars moves into Capricorn, the sign of its exaltation, and activates your sixth house. This can be an extremely productive period for finishing projects, organizing obligations, starting long-term work and taking on more responsibility. For many Leos the end of the year may be intense at work, but also important for building more stable professional foundations.

## Money and material security

Money is one of the more significant themes of 2027.

At the very start Mars moves through your second house, so you may face higher expenses, investments or the need to settle an earlier financial question.

From 10 January to 21 February, while Mars moves retrograde through Virgo, it is especially important to watch how you handle money.

Some Leos could have unplanned expenses, costs connected with work, the home or certain obligations they postponed earlier. A return to old financial agreements, debts or investments that did not bring the expected results is also possible.

This is not a period for hasty decisions just because you want to solve a certain problem as soon as possible. It is especially important not to let optimism lead you to spend beyond your real means.

Jupiter in your sign can heighten the wish for luxury, pleasures, travel and purchases that give you a sense of success. Material security does not depend only on the size of your income, but also on how you manage what you have.

Mercury, as ruler of your second and eleventh houses, has three retrograde cycles during the year:

- from 9 February to 3 March,
- from 10 June to 4 July,
- from 7 to 28 October.

In these periods it is useful to double-check financial agreements, documents, payments, deadlines and the terms of business collaborations.

This does not mean money will not come, only that more attention needs to go to the details.

The first significant shift in finances may be felt from the second half of May, when Mars moves through your second house again, encouraging more initiative around earnings.

An especially important period begins on 26 July, with Jupiter entering Virgo.

Its stay in your second house can open opportunities over the following months for higher income, extra work, improving your business or better management of material resources.

From 20 August to 14 September Venus further activates your second house.

This is a favorable period for financial conversations, negotiating pay, collecting payment for earlier work and investing in what has long-term value. In September Jupiter forms a square with Uranus, so unexpected financial circumstances connected with friends, groups or joint ventures may also arise.

That is why it is not wise to rely only on the assumption that future income will cover all present expenses.

The year can bring significant financial growth, but it will ask you to learn to tell apart passing pleasure from real material security.

## Love and partnership

Love and partnership are among the deepest and most important themes of 2027.

Pluto continues its long stay in Aquarius, your seventh house, which is connected with marriage, serious partnerships and relationships with other people. This transit is not only about changes in your love life.

It symbolizes a much deeper process through which you may begin to re-examine what closeness, trust, equality and a shared life really mean to you.

Some Leos could notice that they no longer agree to relationships in which they do not feel respected enough. Others may feel a need to take an existing relationship to a more serious level.

Pluto can also raise questions of control, jealousy, possessiveness or an imbalance of power in partnerships.

That is why it is important this year not to try to keep love alive by your will alone. A partnership involves two people, their needs, their responsibilities and their willingness to build a future together.

For single Leos the first interesting period opens as early as 7 January to 3 February, when Venus moves through your fifth house of love, falling in love and emotional fulfillment. This can be a period of new acquaintances, flirting, socializing and a greater need for emotional excitement. You may meet someone who catches your attention with their energy, optimism or different view of life.

From 3 February to 1 March new romantic acquaintances may also arise through work or daily activities. February is an especially important period for partnerships. The solar eclipse of 6 February in Aquarius activates your seventh house.

This period can symbolically open a new chapter in partnerships.

For some Leos it may mean an important decision about a relationship, marriage or a shared future. For others a need may arise to re-examine a relationship that has not been working well for a long time.

From 1 to 26 March Venus moves through your seventh house, further emphasizing love, reconciliation, agreements and partnerships. For single Leos this can be an interesting period for entering a relationship.

For those already in a relationship, questions of shared plans, agreements or resolving earlier disagreements may come up.

From 26 March to 20 April Venus moves through your eighth house.

This period can heighten the need for deeper emotional connection, intimacy and trust.

Some Leos could face their own fears of loss, rejection or emotional vulnerability. It is exactly through honest conversations and a willingness to show your feelings that you can achieve greater closeness.

The second half of the year brings another significant period.

From 27 July to 20 August Venus moves through your sign.

This is one of the loveliest periods for personal attractiveness, self-confidence, socializing, new acquaintances and enjoying life. Many Leos may want to change their look, give more attention to themselves or simply feel comfortable in their own skin again.

The solar eclipse of 2 August in Leo can symbolize an important personal beginning.

Although it relates to your identity, it can also affect the way you enter relationships. You may realize that you no longer want to be the same person in love. You may begin to set boundaries differently, or choose a relationship that is more in tune with your real needs.

The lunar eclipse of 17 August in Aquarius further activates your seventh house, so August can be especially significant for the relationship between you and your partner.

For some Leos this may be a period of important agreements, and for others a moment when it becomes clearer what they want from a shared future.

From 16 October to 25 November Mars moves through your fifth house.

This transit can heighten passion, emotional initiative and the need to fall in love, but also the wish to spend more time with someone who matters to you.

An especially interesting period opens from early November to 25 November, when Venus also moves through your fifth house. For single Leos this can be a very active period in love. For those in a relationship, November can bring more shared activities, travel, socializing and a need to bring spontaneity and pleasure back into the relationship. Remember that strong attraction is not the same as emotional security.

In 2027 you could learn that a true relationship does not require you to keep proving your worth.

## Family, home and property

Family circumstances and the question of living space can be especially emphasized at several points in 2027. At the start of the year Venus moves through your fourth house, until 7 January.

This can bring a need to spend more time with family, arrange your living space or settle certain questions about the home.

Some Leos could think about moving, buying furniture or creating more pleasant conditions for everyday life. From 2 September to 16 October Mars moves through Scorpio, your fourth house. This is one of the more active periods for the home. Renovation work, adapting your space, investing in property or settling practical housing questions are possible. Mars in the fourth house can also bring a tenser family atmosphere. You may feel that too many obligations fall on you, or that family members do not share the same opinion about certain decisions. It is especially important not to let accumulated dissatisfaction turn into unnecessary conflicts.

From 8 October to 1 November Venus moves through your fourth house again, opening a more favorable chance for agreements, decorating the home and improving family relationships.

Because Mercury is retrograde in October, certain work, purchases or administrative matters connected with property may need extra checking.

For some Leos 2027 may be a year in which they realize that changing their living space is not only a practical question. Sometimes we also need to change the way we function within the family.

Throughout 2027 Saturn and Neptune stay in Aries, your ninth house. This is one of the more important areas of life for Leo, especially when it comes to university, higher education, foreign countries, long journeys, legal matters, philosophy of life and long-term plans.

Saturn in the ninth house can symbolize a more serious attitude toward education, a need for further training, passing exams or gaining certain qualifications.

For some Leos this may be a period in which they decide to continue their education or finish something they started earlier. Saturn asks for discipline, perseverance and a willingness to achieve certain results gradually. Neptune in the same house brings different symbolism.

It can encourage an interest in spirituality, psychology, philosophy, art or fields that widen your way of thinking. On the other hand, it can also bring unrealistic expectations about moving abroad, education or achieving some big life plan.

That is why it is especially important during the year to check information, enrollment conditions, documents, legal obligations and deadlines.

There is no need to give up on big plans, but they need to be put on realistic foundations.

From 20 April to 14 May Venus moves through your ninth house, which can be a favorable period for planning trips, contacts abroad, educational activities and working with people from other backgrounds. Pleasant circumstances connected with a trip, or meeting someone from another city or country, are also possible.

From 15 July to 2 September Mars moves through your third house. Since Mars rules your ninth house, this transit links long journeys with daily obligations, communication, paperwork and short trips.

In this period some Leos could travel more often, organize business meetings, deal with documents or be in contact with people abroad. Extra caution is needed in traffic, especially if you travel tired, under pressure or in a hurry.

From January to early April, while Mars is retrograde, it is also useful to pay more attention to organizing long journeys. Check documents, bookings, costs and deadlines. In the second half of the year Saturn's retrograde from August to December may reopen certain questions about education, foreign countries or long-term life plans.

You may then have to change your approach or postpone certain decisions. A delay does not have to be a sign of failure. Sometimes it allows us to prepare more seriously for what we want to achieve.

Throughout 2027 Uranus moves through Gemini, your eleventh house.

This transit can bring changes in your social circle, friendships, groups and long-term plans. Many Leos could meet people who think differently from their usual circle. Getting involved in new communities, professional groups, online projects or activities connected with modern technology is possible.

Uranus in the eleventh house can bring unexpected acquaintances, but also sudden changes in existing friendships. You may realize that you no longer share the same interests with certain people. Others may feel a need for more freedom within their friendships. An especially interesting period opens from 8 June to 3 July, when Venus moves through your eleventh house. This can be a good time for socializing, meeting new people, joint projects and achieving plans with the support of friends or colleagues. During Mercury retrograde from 10 June to 4 July certain agreements may need further adjustment. Some old contacts may come back to life.

You may renew a collaboration with someone with whom you earlier failed to carry out a certain plan. This year Uranus reminds you that you do not have to build the future only with people who have always been part of your life. Sometimes it is exactly a new acquaintance that can open a completely different view of the opportunities ahead of you.

## Health and daily habits

In 2027 it is important to pay more attention to how you divide your energy.

In the first half of the year Jupiter in your first house can symbolize more vitality, optimism and a need for a more active way of life. Jupiter can also encourage excess. For some Leos this may show as a bigger appetite, changes in body weight, overindulgence in food or a tendency to take on more obligations than the body can handle.

From 21 February to mid-May Mars moves through your sign. This period can bring a greater need for movement, physical activity, sport and achieving personal goals. Because Mars is retrograde until early April, it is important not to push your body and not to try to achieve everything at once. Pay special attention to physical activity, warming up, rest and avoiding unnecessary risk.

Mars in the first house can symbolically increase a tendency toward impulsive reactions, so it is useful to be more careful with physical exertion and hasty activities.

Saturn, as ruler of your sixth house, stays in your ninth house all year. That is why daily habits may be connected with travel, learning, work obligations and changes in your pace of life. You may sometimes feel that you do not have enough time for yourself.

From 26 July Jupiter moves into your second house, which further emphasizes the need for moderation, especially when it comes to food, enjoyment and personal habits.

At the end of November and in December Mars moves through your sixth house.

This can be a period of a heavier workload, more obligations and a more intense daily pace. Although you may have a lot of energy for work, it is important not to neglect the need for rest.

## Closing message

The year can give you many chances to progress, achieve your ambitions and change the way you see your own possibilities.

Perhaps this very year will teach you that success does not always mean having more.

More money, more obligations, more recognition or more people around you. Sometimes the greatest success is learning to recognize what we truly need.

In the first half of the year Jupiter will give you symbolic support for personal growth, development and wider horizons. You may want to achieve something you have long put off.

You may get the chance to stand out, change jobs, start a new project or finally make a decision you did not have enough confidence for before. Do not let big expectations take you away from reality. Not every opportunity is equally good. Not every business success is worth giving up your private life for.

And not every relationship with strong attraction is also a relationship with security. In 2027 you could learn a lot about your limits, wishes and real priorities. Pluto in your seventh house keeps changing the way you see partnership.

Saturn and Neptune in your ninth house encourage you to think more seriously about the future, education, foreign countries and your beliefs. Uranus in your eleventh house can bring people and circumstances that will change your plans so far.

And from the end of July Jupiter will begin to emphasize material security, personal values and the results of everything you built before. That is why this year can be a transition from one period of personal growth into a period in which it will be important to turn all your ideas and abilities into something concrete. You may change the way you earn. You may see love differently. You may distance yourself from people with whom you no longer share goals. And perhaps, for the first time in a long while, you will decide to put your own needs before other people's expectations.

Leo is a sign that naturally wants to shine, create, lead and leave its mark.

2027 can teach you that you do not have to be the center of attention all the time to be important. You do not have to prove your worth to everyone. And you do not have to win every battle to know how strong you are.

Your greatest opportunity in 2027 may not lie in how much you achieve, but in how much you learn to value yourself, your time, your abilities and the people who are truly by your side.

Because when you know what you are worth, you will much more easily recognize what you deserve.
