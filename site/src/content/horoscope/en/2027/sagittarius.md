*For Sagittarius by Sun sign and rising sign*

Dear Sagittarius,

The year ahead raises an important question: how ready are you to change the direction you are going in so that you can achieve what you truly want?

You are a sign that naturally strives for freedom, exploration, new experiences, knowledge and wider horizons. Ahead of you is a year that can teach you that progress does not always mean constant movement. Sometimes you need to go back a few steps to see the road ahead more clearly.

You do not enter 2027 feeling that all the stories of the past are finished. Jupiter, your ruler, is in Leo, your ninth house, but it will be retrograde until 13 April. That is why the first months of the year may be marked by re-examining earlier decisions and returning to unfinished business, education, travel or plans that were not carried out in the previous year.

You may feel that certain situations are repeating, that you are waiting for answers or that circumstances are not yet clear enough for you to set off in a completely new direction.

Jupiter retrograde does not have to be an obstacle. It can give you the chance to finish what you started earlier, renew certain contacts or see differently the opportunities that were already available to you. Higher education, professional training, foreign countries, long journeys and legal matters are especially emphasized.

For some Sagittarians this may be a year of finishing university, passing important exams, gaining additional qualifications or restarting plans connected with living and working abroad.

From 13 April, when Jupiter turns direct, you may feel more optimism, self-confidence and readiness to make your plans concrete. One of the most significant moments of the year comes at the end of July. Jupiter entering Virgo on 26 July activates your tenth house, which is connected with career, professional status, ambition and social standing.

A new stage of growth then begins, in which many Sagittarians will have the chance to improve their professional position, expand their business or reach goals they have long worked toward. At the same time, Saturn and Neptune in Aries, your fifth house, emphasize questions of love, children, creativity and personal fulfillment, while Uranus in Gemini brings changes in partnerships.

That is why 2027 will not be focused only on professional growth. Ahead of you is a period in which you will have to find a balance between personal wishes, emotional needs, relationships with others and the ambitions you want to achieve.

## Work and career

Work could become one of the most significant themes of 2027, especially from the second half of the year. The start of the year may call for a bit more patience. Mars, which rules your fifth and twelfth houses, moves retrograde from January to early April, through Virgo and Leo.

Its retrograde through your tenth house can symbolize a re-examination of professional decisions, relationships with superiors, earlier business projects and the direction in which you want to develop your career. Some Sagittarians may return to work they did earlier, renew collaboration with former colleagues or reconsider an offer they turned down in the previous period. There may also be situations in which you have to correct certain oversights, finish projects you started or take responsibility for decisions you made earlier. Since Mars also rules your twelfth house, some business activities may take place away from the public eye.

This may be preparing an important project, working on an idea you do not yet want to present to others, or a need to keep certain professional plans to yourself until you are sure how they will develop. At the same time, Mars as ruler of your fifth house can link your career with creative projects, independent work and jobs that call for personal initiative. That is why the start of the year is not necessarily unfavorable, but it may call for reorganization and a different approach to work obligations.

From 7 January to 3 February Venus moves through your sign.

Since Venus rules your sixth house of work and your eleventh house of groups, friendships and long-term plans, this period can bring support from colleagues, favorable business contacts or a chance to get an interesting professional offer through acquaintances.

Contacts abroad, with international organizations and with people who share your professional interests may be especially significant.

From 14 May to 8 June, when Venus moves through Taurus, your sixth house, the atmosphere at work may become more pleasant. This can be a favorable period for improving relationships with colleagues, organizing daily obligations, talking about working conditions or finding a job.

For Sagittarians who have their own business, this period can bring useful collaborations and a chance to improve the way they work.

From 8 June to 3 July, with Venus in Gemini, business partnerships, negotiations, contracts and client relationships are emphasized. During part of this period Mercury will be retrograde, so it is important to pay extra attention to the terms of collaboration, agreements and documents. The most significant turn at work comes at the end of July, when Jupiter enters Virgo. Jupiter is your ruler, so its move into your tenth house can symbolize significant professional growth. For some Sagittarians this may be a chance of advancement, changing jobs, moving to a more responsible position or starting their own business. Those who invested in education, gained experience or developed certain skills in previous years may now get the chance to use their knowledge more concretely. The period from 20 August to 14 September stands out, when Venus will also move through Virgo.

The symbolism of Jupiter and Venus then comes together in your house of career, which can bring useful business contacts, support from influential people, greater professional visibility or more favorable circumstances for achieving your ambitions. Jupiter in Virgo also calls for a practical approach.

Big plans are not enough. You will need to show organization, precision, responsibility and a willingness to attend to details.

It is exactly through daily work, further training and perseverance that you can achieve results of long-term significance.

## Love

Love and partnership can be one of the most dynamic areas of life in 2027.

Saturn and Neptune continue their stay in Aries, your fifth house, which is connected with falling in love, romantic experiences, pleasure, creativity and children. This combination can bring very different emotional experiences.

Neptune can heighten the need for an ideal love, romantic experiences and relationships in which you feel a special connection with the other person. At the same time there may be a tendency to see certain people through your own expectations rather than through what they really show.

Saturn, on the other hand, asks for seriousness, responsibility and clearer emotional boundaries.

That is why some Sagittarians may begin to re-examine what they actually expect from a relationship and how ready they are to invest in a relationship that has no clearly defined future. Mars retrograde from January to early April, as ruler of your fifth house, can further activate earlier emotional stories.

Contact with former partners, meeting people you once had feelings for or reopening questions that never found a final resolution are possible. The possibility of love and work overlapping is especially interesting. Some Sagittarians could renew contact with someone they know through work, while others may re-examine a relationship in which private and professional circumstances were intertwined.

From 7 January to 3 February, with Venus in your sign, you may feel more self-confidence, personal attractiveness and a need to focus on your own wishes. This is a period in which you can more easily attract other people's attention, renew social contacts or simply want to bring more pleasure into everyday life.

For single Sagittarians the period from 20 April to 14 May stands out, when Venus moves through Aries, your fifth house. New acquaintances, falling in love, romantic encounters and more exciting emotional experiences are then possible. Because Saturn and Neptune are in the same house, it will be important to tell apart momentary attraction from a relationship that has the potential to grow.

From 8 June to 3 July Venus in Gemini activates your seventh house, which is connected with marriage, serious relationships and partnership.

This period can bring a need for more closeness, shared plans and settling questions that matter for the future of the relationship. Uranus stays in Gemini, your seventh house, all year and brings a completely different dynamic. Uranus in the seventh house can symbolize unexpected changes in partnerships, a need for more freedom and a different way of connecting with others.

For some Sagittarians this may be a sudden acquaintance with someone very different from their earlier partners. For those already in a relationship or marriage, Uranus can raise questions of personal freedom, shared expectations and the need to adapt the relationship to changed circumstances in life. This does not mean every relationship has to go through a crisis or a breakup.

Relationships with enough mutual understanding can get the chance to develop on a different footing. From 14 September to 8 October, with Venus in Libra, interesting acquaintances through friends, social gatherings, shared activities or online are possible. From 8 October to 1 November, when Venus moves through Scorpio, your twelfth house, relationships may become more complicated. Secret crushes, relationships that are not public, jealousy, mistrust or a need to keep certain feelings to yourself may come to life. It is important not to enter relationships that are too unclear from the very start.

From 1 to 25 November Venus returns to your sign and brings another period in which you can give more attention to yourself, love and personal fulfillment. The most important love lesson of 2027 is that freedom and closeness do not have to exclude each other.

A real relationship should not limit you, but neither should it keep you in constant uncertainty.

## Money

In 2027 money can be significantly linked with your professional growth, especially in the second half of the year.

At the start of January the Sun and Mercury are in Capricorn, your second house, which is connected with personal income, earnings, property and your attitude toward material security. That is why the first months of the year may focus on re-examining financial plans, earlier expenses and the way you handle money.

From 3 February to 1 March Venus moves through Capricorn, further emphasizing financial themes. This can be an interesting period for collecting payment for earlier work, receiving expected income or finding an additional source of earnings.

Since Venus rules your sixth and eleventh houses, financial opportunities may be connected with daily work, colleagues, business groups or projects you take part in together with other people. Some Sagittarians could get financial support through work, agree on better terms of collaboration or earn income from activities they started earlier.

From 3 to 27 July, when Venus moves through Cancer, your eighth house, shared finances are further activated. These may be questions of loans, debts, taxes, contributions, inheritance, a partner's income or money you manage together with another person. For some Sagittarians this period can bring a chance of financial support, a more favorable agreement or considering an investment. It is important not to make decisions based only on expected profit. An especially significant change comes with Jupiter entering your tenth house at the end of July.

Professional growth can open the possibility of higher income, advancement or earning through new business responsibilities. From 25 November, when Venus enters Capricorn, your second house, again, financial questions come to the fore once more.

## Health and daily life

In 2027 an important theme will be finding a balance between professional obligations, personal needs and the time you devote to rest. Sagittarius naturally strives for activity, movement and change, but this year may call for a somewhat more serious approach to daily habits.

Especially in the first months, while Mars is retrograde, you may feel that certain jobs take more time and energy than you expected. This can be a good moment to re-examine the way you organize your obligations and assess how much room you leave for rest. Saturn in your fifth house symbolically emphasizes the need to take what brings you pleasure more seriously.

You may notice that you no longer have the same need for certain activities, or that you want to use your free time better. From 14 May to 8 June, when Venus moves through Taurus, your sixth house, your daily rhythm may become more pleasant.

This is a good period for adopting useful habits, organizing your work better, recreation and paying attention to your quality of life. From the end of July, when Jupiter enters Virgo, work may intensify significantly. That is exactly why it will be important not to pursue professional ambitions at the cost of your own rest. Regular sleep, physical activity, a balanced diet and preventive checkups can be an important part of a more responsible relationship with yourself.

## Friendships, social life and a change of surroundings

In 2027 social life can play a significant role in your personal and professional growth. Venus, as ruler of your eleventh house, activates different areas of life during the year, linking friendships, groups and social contacts with work, money, love and education.

Already from 7 January, when Venus enters your sign, you may feel a greater need to socialize, exchange ideas and connect with people who inspire you. Some Sagittarians may renew contact with friends they have not seen for a long time, or make acquaintances that will later matter for work. Throughout 2027 Pluto stays in Aquarius, your third house, and symbolically changes the way you communicate with others.

You may become much more careful in choosing the people with whom you share your plans, thoughts and private information. Some relationships may become deeper, while for others you will realize you no longer need to keep them up out of habit.

From 14 September to 8 October, when Venus moves through Libra, your eleventh house, social life can become especially active. This is a period of interesting acquaintances, gatherings, joint projects and a chance to make useful contacts through friends.

For single Sagittarians the social circle can also become a place of new emotional experiences. During the year it will be important to tell apart people who truly support your growth from those connected to you only by present circumstances.

You do not have to belong to every group or take part in every shared plan.

Sometimes changing the direction of your life also means changing the people with whom you share that path.

Jupiter, your ruler, stays in Leo, your ninth house, until the end of July. The ninth house is connected with higher education, foreign countries, long journeys, legal matters and wider horizons.

Since Jupiter is retrograde until 13 April, the first months of the year can be especially important for finishing education you started earlier, passing remaining exams or reconsidering plans connected with going abroad.

Some Sagittarians may decide to continue education they interrupted earlier, enroll in further training or return to the idea of living and working in another country.

For students this can be a significant year. Jupiter in the ninth house supports gaining knowledge, developing academic interests and widening professional expertise.

From 13 April, when Jupiter turns direct, many plans may get a clearer direction. You may then get the answers you were waiting for, start certain procedures or organize a trip more concretely.

From 1 to 26 March, when Venus moves through Aquarius, your third house, communication, short trips, training and contact with people close to you are emphasized.

This can be a pleasant period for learning, exchanging information, short trips or making useful acquaintances.

Throughout the year Pluto in Aquarius brings deeper changes in your way of thinking. You may become interested in fields you did not pay much attention to before, or decide to develop certain knowledge at a more serious level. Technology, digital communication, research and modern forms of education may be especially significant.

From 27 July to 20 August, with Venus in Leo, long journeys, foreign countries and higher education are emphasized again.

For many Sagittarians this can be one of the more pleasant periods for travel, a holiday abroad, meeting people from other countries or taking part in educational programs.

The periods of Mercury retrograde call for special attention during the year.

Mercury rules your seventh and tenth houses, so its retrograde can symbolically affect partnerships, business agreements, contracts and legal matters.

Mercury will be retrograde:

- from 9 February to 3 March,
- from 10 June to 4 July,
- from 7 to 28 October.

During these periods it is useful to double-check documents, terms of collaboration, business agreements and information you get from others.

It is especially important to be careful with promises, deadlines, signing contracts and starting legal proceedings. This does not mean every important decision should be postponed, only that you need to act thoughtfully, with verified information and appropriate professional advice.

## Home, family and private life

Although professional ambitions and relationships with others will be very emphasized, your private life will also hold an important place in 2027.

From 26 March to 20 April Venus moves through Pisces, your fourth house, which is connected with home, family, property and a sense of personal security. This period can bring a greater need to spend time with family, arrange your living space or settle certain questions about household obligations.

Some Sagittarians could think about redecorating, buying furniture or creating a more pleasant environment for everyday life.

Family meetings, gatherings and conversations that contribute to better mutual understanding are also possible. As your tenth house of career is emphasized from the second half of the year, it will be important to find a balance between professional ambitions and private needs.

A heavier workload may call for organizing family life differently.

For some Sagittarians the support of family will be an important anchor during professional changes. Do not forget that success is not only what you achieve in the outside world. How you feel when you come home after all your obligations matters too.

## Closing message

2027 opens opportunities ahead of you that you may not be able to recognize clearly at the very beginning. The first months may bring you back to earlier plans, unfinished business, relationships and decisions you thought you had left behind.

You may feel that certain things are moving more slowly than you would like. What seems like a standstill at the start of the year you may later understand as an important part of preparing for a new stage of life. Until mid-April you will need to re-examine your decisions, finish what you started and see more clearly the direction you want to take. From spring you may feel more optimism, self-confidence and readiness to open up to new opportunities.

And from the end of July, when your ruler Jupiter moves into your house of career, one of the more significant periods of professional growth may begin for many Sagittarians.

This year will not only teach you how to achieve success. It will also teach you to recognize relationships that deserve your time, how important personal freedom is to you and what fulfillment really means to you.

Some relationships may change, certain plans may take a different direction, and what you once considered important may no longer have the same value. You are a sign that naturally believes there is a new opportunity, a different path and a wider perspective somewhere.

In 2027 you will have the chance to use this very quality of yours in a much more concrete way. You do not have to keep searching for something new in order to progress. Sometimes it is enough to recognize the value of what you have already learned, gained and built.

2027 teaches you that true freedom is not only the possibility of taking any path, but also the ability to consciously choose the one that leads you toward the life you want.
