*For Taurus by Sun sign and rising sign*

Dear Taurus,

Ahead of you is a year that can bring significant changes in how you see yourself, your relationships, your work and your material security. After several years of change, uncertainty and circumstances that often pulled you out of familiar frames, 2027 brings a chance to see your future differently.

You do not enter the new year completely unburdened.

In the autumn of 2026 your ruler Venus went through a retrograde cycle, bringing you back to certain emotional experiences, relationships, financial decisions and questions of personal values.

That is why the start of 2027 may be a continuation of a process that began the previous year.

You may feel that some relationships have not been fully cleared up, that certain business circumstances need further review or that you are not yet quite sure which way you want to go.

Until 7 January Venus stays in Scorpio, your seventh house of partnership, where it is traditionally not in its most favorable dignity. Symbolically, the start of the year can bring greater emotional sensitivity, exhaustion from earlier experiences or the need to put certain relationships on a different footing.

This can apply especially to partnerships, business collaborations and situations in which you previously directed too much energy toward other people.

From 7 January, when Venus moves into Sagittarius, the atmosphere gradually changes. You may feel more optimism, a wish to break out of certain limits and a need to look again at the opportunities available to you.

This is not a year to make decisions based only on momentary enthusiasm.

Throughout the year Saturn and Neptune stay in your twelfth house, emphasizing the closing of certain life stages, a re-examination of the past and the need for greater inner stability. Saturn teaches you to recognize your own limits clearly, while Neptune raises questions of intuition, trust, uncertainty and everything you may have been suppressing for a long time.

During the year you may realize that certain people, habits or circumstances no longer have the same place in your life. A change in your attitude toward work and money will be especially important.

Uranus in Gemini moves through your second house, while Pluto in Aquarius activates your tenth house of career and social standing. Their connection emphasizes a long period of professional and material transformation.

For many Taurus natives 2027 may be a year in which they change not only their job or the way they earn, but also their idea of what success and security mean to them.

## Work and career – a change of professional direction

Work is one of the most important themes of 2027. Pluto continues its transit through Aquarius, your tenth house, emphasizing a long-term transformation of your career, professional status and relationship with authority.

This process does not have to bring changes overnight.

It may be a period in which you gradually realize that you no longer want to work under the same conditions, in the same environment or in the way you did in previous years. Some Taurus natives could consider changing jobs, leaving their profession, starting their own business or taking on a more responsible position.

It is especially interesting that Uranus, which is connected with your tenth house, moves through your second house of money and personal values. This symbolism links professional changes with the question of earnings.

You may realize that your work so far has not been valued enough, or that you want to develop a different way of earning income. Some Taurus natives may become interested in modern technology, digital business, working online or occupations that call for more flexibility.

15 June stands out, when Uranus and Pluto form a trine, emphasizing the symbolism of a change in professional direction, new technologies and a different attitude toward material security.

For some Taurus natives this may be an incentive to consider more seriously an idea they have been thinking about for a long time.

The start of the year may bring the need to clarify certain business relationships, collaborations or earlier agreements.

Venus, your ruler and ruler of your sixth house of work, moves through your seventh house of partnership until 7 January.

That is why work questions may be connected with colleagues, clients or people with whom you have certain contractual obligations.

From 7 January to 3 February Venus in your eighth house emphasizes shared finances, investments, business resources and the need to change the way you work. You may consider additional investments, reorganizing your finances or a different business system.

Because Jupiter and Mars are retrograde, a careful review of bigger decisions is advised. There is no need to give up on plans, but it is important to check whether they are sustainable in the long run.

From 3 February to 1 March Venus moves through Capricorn, your ninth house. This is an interesting period for Taurus natives who are studying, preparing for exams, taking extra courses or want to improve their professional knowledge.

It is important to be persistent and take your obligations seriously.

This period can also be interesting for working with partners abroad, contacts with foreign companies or planning business trips.

From 1 to 26 March Venus moves into Aquarius, your tenth house of career.

This opens more favorable symbolism for presenting yourself professionally, new contacts, conversations with superiors and showing your abilities. Some Taurus natives may get more attention at work or the chance to present a project they have been working on.

During Mercury retrograde, until 3 March, it is important to check the details and define agreements clearly.

From 26 March to 20 April Venus moves through your eleventh house. This can be an interesting period for professional contacts, joint projects, teamwork and connecting with people who have similar plans. Some business opportunities may come through friends, acquaintances or former colleagues.

From 20 April to 14 May Venus moves through your twelfth house.

In this period you can give more attention to preparing projects, research and finishing tasks that are not yet ready to be made public. Avoid unclear business agreements and situations in which you do not have enough information.

From 14 May to 8 June Venus stays in your sign. This period can be especially significant for personal and professional growth. You may feel a stronger need to stand out, present your ideas or change the way others see you.

Taurus natives who work with clients, run a private business or want to start their own project can use this period to present themselves professionally.

From 8 June to 3 July Venus moves into your second house of money, so business activities may be linked with more concrete financial results. From 15 July to 2 September Mars moves through your sixth house.

This is a period of more intense work, more obligations and a need for discipline. Changes in your work team, new duties or situations that call for quick reactions are possible.

Some Taurus natives may feel that more is expected of them than before. It is important not to get into unnecessary conflicts with colleagues and to set clear boundaries.

From 14 September to 8 October Venus moves through your sixth house, which can contribute to a more pleasant atmosphere at work. Interesting business contacts, better working conditions, recognition for earlier effort or talks about a promotion are possible.

The end of the year, especially from 25 November, emphasizes education, foreign countries and long-term professional plans.

The most important work message of 2027 is that you no longer automatically agree to conditions that do not match your abilities and goals.

## Love – the return of the past and new emotional experiences

The love life of Taurus goes through several very different stages in 2027. The start of the year may be marked by a re-examination of emotional relationships from the previous year. Venus, your ruler, stays in your seventh house of partnership until 7 January. Because of the earlier retrograde cycle, some Taurus natives may need to clarify relationships that were left unfinished.

You may think about someone you lost touch with, a relationship that did not meet your expectations or a person for whom your feelings have not fully faded. Mars, ruler of your seventh house of partnership and your twelfth house of hidden circumstances, turns retrograde on 10 January in Virgo, your fifth house of love.

This symbolism is especially interesting when it comes to former partners, earlier crushes and emotional stories that never found a final resolution. Renewed contact with someone from the past is possible. Some Taurus natives may also need to keep a relationship out of the public eye for a while.

The return of someone from the past does not necessarily mean the return of the same love.

Sometimes certain relationships come back to life so that we can understand how much we have changed in the meantime.

From 21 February Mars returns to Leo, your fourth house, so attention turns to family, life together and emotional security.

For Taurus natives in a relationship this can be a period of conversations about where to live, family obligations or changes in a shared home.

From 26 March to 20 April Venus moves through Pisces, your eleventh house. This is one of the more interesting periods for social life, new acquaintances and contacts through friends. Venus in Pisces is in the sign of its exaltation, so its symbolism is linked with romance, tenderness and the idealization of love. Some Taurus natives could meet an interesting person through friends, social events or shared activities. A friendship may also grow into something more. It is important not to idealize someone you do not yet know well enough.

From 20 April to 14 May Venus moves through your twelfth house, so emotional relationships may become more discreet. Secret crushes, relationships not yet presented to others or a re-examination of feelings you are not ready to show openly are possible.

From 14 May to 8 June Venus stays in Taurus. This is an especially lovely period for personal fulfillment, self-confidence and the need to give yourself more attention. You may want to change your look, refresh your wardrobe, socialize more or simply find time for activities you enjoy. Your attractiveness and openness to new acquaintances may be more pronounced. For Taurus natives in a relationship this can be a period of bringing back closeness and shared pleasures.

From 14 May to 15 July Mars moves through your fifth house of love again.

This period can bring more initiative, passion, new acquaintances and emotional themes coming to life. For Taurus natives who have children, much of their attention may go to the children's needs, activities, education or shared plans.

From 27 July to 20 August Venus moves through your fourth house. Family relationships, life together and the need for security then become more pronounced.

From 20 August to 14 September Venus moves through your fifth house of love. This is one of the loveliest periods of the year for new acquaintances, romance, creativity, hobbies and emotional fulfillment. It can be especially interesting for single Taurus natives, while those in a relationship may want to bring more romance into it.

From 2 September to 16 October Mars moves through Scorpio, your seventh house of partnership. Mars in Scorpio is in its traditional domicile, so partnership themes may intensify. This can bring more passion and initiative, but also tension if certain problems have long gone unresolved. Arguments, jealousy, a need for control or disagreements about joint decisions are possible. That is why it will be important to avoid competing with your partner or trying to change them to fit your own expectations.

From 8 October to 1 November Venus also moves through your seventh house. This period can bring a chance to grow closer, important conversations and a clearer definition of the relationship.

From 25 November Mars moves into Capricorn, your ninth house. Since Mars rules your seventh house of partnership, love themes may be linked with foreign countries, long journeys, education or people who live in another country. Meeting someone while traveling or contact with a person from a different background is possible.

For some Taurus natives this may be the beginning of a more serious relationship that calls for planning and long-term commitment.

## Money

Money is one of the key themes of 2027.

Uranus in your second house brings the symbolism of change in how you earn, in your personal values and in your attitude toward material security. Taurus naturally strives for stability, but this year you may realize that old ways of doing business no longer bring the results you want.

Some may consider additional sources of income, digital business, new technologies or a change of professional direction.

Mercury, ruler of your second house of money, has three retrograde periods in 2027:

- from 9 February to 3 March,
- from 10 June to 4 July,
- from 7 to 28 October.

In those periods extra care is advised when signing contracts, in financial negotiations, purchases and when planning investments. This does not mean every decision will be unfavorable, only that it is useful to leave more time to check the information.

It is especially interesting that Mercury also rules your fifth house, so financial themes may be linked with children, creative projects, hobbies and emotional pleasures. Jupiter, ruler of your eighth house of shared money, loans and investments, moves through your fourth house of home and property until 26 July.

This symbolism links financial decisions with housing, family property, renovation or buying property. Jupiter is retrograde until 13 April. That is why I would single out the first half of the year, especially the period until mid-April, as a time to review earlier plans, check documents and realistically assess your financial possibilities.

If you are thinking about buying an apartment or a house, or about a larger investment, it is important to have a clear picture of the total costs and long-term obligations. From mid-April there may be more room to turn plans prepared earlier into concrete steps.

The most interesting financial periods:

From 8 June to 3 July Venus moves through your second house, which symbolically emphasizes earnings, a sense of security and valuing your own work.

From 14 September to 8 October Venus in your sixth house links money with work, employment and the chance of more favorable business terms.

From 1 to 25 November Venus moves through your eighth house, so shared money, investments and financial agreements with other people are emphasized.

In the autumn it is especially important not to take on financial obligations based only on expected future income.

## Friendships, groups and social life

In 2027 you may notice that your attitude toward certain friends and social groups is gradually changing. Saturn and Neptune in your twelfth house symbolically emphasize the closing of old life patterns, withdrawal and the need to choose more carefully the people you give your trust to. Neptune, as the modern indicator connected with your eleventh house, can raise questions of idealized friendships or disappointment in certain people.

You may realize that certain relationships were not what you imagined them to be. This does not have to mean conflict or a break, simply a change of priorities.

From 26 March to 20 April Venus moves through your eleventh house, opening opportunities for new acquaintances, socializing and connecting with people who share your interests.

On 26 July Jupiter enters Virgo, your fifth house, so in the second half of the year the need for pleasure, creativity and better use of your free time grows stronger.

In 2027 you can learn that it is not how many people you have around you that matters, but how accepted and free you feel in their company.

## Health and daily life

Throughout the year Saturn and Neptune in your twelfth house symbolically emphasize the need to devote yourself more to inner peace, rest and releasing yourself from excessive burdens. Taurus often has the ability to persevere with certain obligations for a long time, even when feeling the need for a break.

2027 may remind you that you do not have to finish everything on your own and be available to others all the time. It is especially important to tell apart the responsibility you truly have from the one you took on out of a sense of duty toward others.

From 14 May to 8 June Venus, ruler of your first and sixth houses, moves through your sign. This period can be a good incentive to give more attention to yourself, your appearance, recreation and daily habits.

If you want to change your style of dress, move more or find activities that suit you, this period can symbolically support such decisions.

From 15 July to 2 September Mars moves through your sixth house of work and daily life. You may then have more obligations and less free time. That is why it will be important to plan your schedule, rest and activities in advance.

From 14 September to 8 October Venus in your sixth house can encourage you to bring more balance into your work environment. The year may not begin with a sense of complete security, but ahead of you is a period in which you can learn a lot about yourself and your own possibilities.

Ahead of you is a year in which you can learn to value your own work more, choose more carefully the people you trust and think more boldly about the future.

Do not be afraid of change just because it was not part of your original plan.

Perhaps exactly what you decide to change in 2027 will become the foundation of a much more stable and fulfilling life in the years to come.
