*For Virgo by Sun sign and rising sign*

Dear Virgo,

Ahead of you is a year that can be one of the more important turning points in your life, especially when it comes to personal growth, professional direction, partnerships and decisions about the future.

2027 will not show all of its possibilities from the very beginning. The first months may be marked by re-examination, finishing earlier obligations, occasional uncertainty and the need to face circumstances you may have been putting off for a long time. Mars, the planet of action, initiative and struggle, is already in your sign at the start of the year, and on 10 January it turns retrograde.

Its stay in your first house can bring heightened activity, impatience, a need to take control of certain situations, but also a feeling that much more is expected of you than before.

It is especially important that Mars rules your eighth house, which astrology connects with big life changes, shared finances, debts, loans, inheritance and circumstances we cannot always influence directly. That is why the start of the year may raise questions that are not only the result of your own decisions.

You may have to adapt to changes in your family surroundings, work circumstances or finances. For some Virgos questions of shared money, earlier investments or obligations that call for a concrete decision may come up.

From 21 February Mars moves back, retrograde, into Leo, your twelfth house, where it stays until mid-May. This period can bring a need to withdraw, re-examine certain decisions, finish something you started earlier or face circumstances that had stayed in the background until then.

You may feel that you are doing a lot, but the results are not visible enough.

You may be tired of repeating the same obligations, or realize that you no longer want to spend energy on things that do not bring you satisfaction. This very period can play an important role in preparing for what comes in the second half of the year. One of the most significant moments for Virgo comes on 26 July 2027, when Jupiter enters your sign. Jupiter, the planet of growth, development, optimism and favorable opportunities, begins its passage through your first house again after about twelve years. This transit can open a completely different chapter of life.

Many people born under your sign could feel a need to change the way they live, improve their work, widen their knowledge, carry out long-term plans or finally start something they did not have enough time, confidence or opportunity for before.

For some Virgos this may be a year of big personal decisions, a change of residence, developing a private business or creating different family circumstances. Jupiter also rules your fourth house, so its entry into your first house can link personal growth with family, home, property and the need to create more secure foundations in life.

It opens opportunities, but how ready you are to recognize and use them will be up to you.

That is why 2027 for Virgo could be divided into two significant periods.

The first, in which you will re-examine old decisions, finish certain obligations and prepare for change. And the second, from the end of July, when you can begin to think much more openly about yourself, your abilities and your future.

This is a year in which you may realize that you do not have to have everything under control in advance to take an important step.

## Work and career

Work can be one of the most dynamic areas of 2027.

Uranus, the planet of change, innovation and unexpected circumstances, continues its stay in Gemini, your tenth house of career, professional status and social standing. This transit symbolizes a long process of change at work. Many Virgos could notice that their attitude toward work has already changed.

You may no longer want to work the same way. Your current work environment may not give you enough room to advance, or you may feel your abilities are not being used enough. For some Virgos a need may arise to become professionally independent, change jobs or move to a more flexible way of working. Uranus also rules your sixth house, which is connected with daily obligations, employment, working conditions and relationships with colleagues.

Its stay in your tenth house links changes in your daily work with your career and social standing.

This means changes do not have to mean only moving to another job.

The way you do your work, the responsibilities you take on or the technologies you use may change. For Virgos who work in digital business, communications, education, technology, writing or online, this transit can open interesting opportunities.

Uranus also brings unpredictability.

That is why it is possible that during the year you will have to adapt to changes you did not plan yourself.

Mercury, your ruler, which also rules your tenth house of career, plays an especially important role.

In 2027 Mercury will be retrograde in three periods:

- from 9 February to 3 March,
- from 10 June to 4 July,
- from 7 to 28 October.

These periods can raise questions of earlier business decisions, unfinished projects, changes to contracts or reconsidering your professional direction.

Some Virgos could again receive an offer for a job they had considered earlier. Others may feel a need to correct certain oversights or organize their work obligations differently. Do not take every delay as a sign that something will not work out.

Sometimes you need to check the information, the terms of collaboration or your own expectations once more.

From 1 to 26 March Venus moves through your sixth house, bringing more favorable symbolism for relationships with colleagues and the daily atmosphere at work. This can be a period of better working conditions, new contacts or more support from colleagues.

An especially interesting period for work opens from 8 June to 3 July, when Venus moves through your tenth house. Venus rules your second house of finances and your ninth house of education, foreign countries and big life plans.

That is why its passage through your tenth house can link professional growth with earnings, further education, working with partners abroad or presenting your work publicly. Some Virgos could receive a business offer, make contact with an important person or begin a collaboration that has the potential to help their professional growth. Since Mercury will be retrograde for part of this period, it is important to check all business agreements carefully.

With Jupiter entering your sign on 26 July, your self-confidence, wish to advance and readiness to take on a more important professional role may grow.

For Virgos who have been thinking about a career change for a long time, this transit can be a significant push. Some could decide to start their own business.

Others may expand existing activities or begin to invest in knowledge that will bring better results in the long run. Not every change is good just because it brings something new. It is important to assess whether a certain business opportunity truly suits your abilities, needs and long-term goals.

In 2027 you could learn that professional success does not only mean having more obligations. Sometimes it is much more important to find work that lets you use your time, knowledge and energy better.

## Money and material security

Money may be one of the more sensitive themes at the start of 2027.

Mars, as ruler of your eighth house, turns retrograde on 10 January while it is in your first house. This symbolism can raise questions of shared finances, debts, loans, inheritance, taxes or obligations you have toward other people.

Some Virgos could face unplanned expenses or the need to settle an earlier financial question. For others the theme of a partner's money, joint investments or financial agreements that need extra attention may come up.

In the first months of the year it is especially important not to make hasty decisions about loans, borrowing and larger investments. Saturn and Neptune stay in your eighth house all year. Saturn can symbolize a need for a more responsible attitude toward financial obligations, while Neptune can bring uncertainty about shared money, promises or expected income. That is why it is not advisable to rely only on verbal agreements or assumptions.

Everything to do with shared money, investments and financial obligations should be clearly defined.

From 14 May to 15 July Mars moves through your first house again and then your second, gradually activating the themes of personal initiative and finances.

Its move into Libra, your second house, on 15 July can increase the need to earn, but also bring extra expenses. Some Virgos could decide to invest money in work, education, equipment or better living conditions. It is especially important not to spend money just because you want to reach a certain goal as soon as possible. Venus, as ruler of your second house, brings several more favorable financial periods during the year.

From 14 May to 8 June it moves through Taurus, your ninth house, which can open opportunities to earn through education, working with partners abroad, travel or work connected with foreign markets. From 8 June to 3 July Venus moves through your tenth house, linking money with professional status and career.

An especially significant period for finances opens from 14 September to 8 October, when Venus moves through Libra, your second house. Venus is in its domicile in Libra, so this transit can symbolize more favorable financial agreements, higher income, collecting payment for earlier work or investing in things that have long-term value.

From 27 July to 20 August Venus moves through your twelfth house. Since it rules your second house, in this period it is useful to pay extra attention to hidden costs, unclear financial agreements or expenses you did not plan. This does not mean a financial loss will necessarily happen, only that you need to handle money more carefully. On 26 July Jupiter enters your sign, which can begin a more favorable period of personal growth. It is important to understand that Jupiter in the first house does not guarantee an automatic rise in income. It can bring opportunities to advance, and it is up to you to turn them into concrete results.

## Love and partnership

Love in 2027 can be very interesting, but also significant for your emotional maturing. Neptune, as ruler of your seventh house, is in your eighth house, which is connected with deep emotional connection, intimacy, trust and shared resources. This symbolism can raise the question of how well you really know the person you are with and how ready you are to share responsibilities with them. Some Virgos could feel a need for a deeper emotional relationship.

For others an existing relationship may come up for review, especially if certain questions have stayed unresolved for a long time.

It is especially important not to see love only through the obligations you take on toward the other person. A partnership should not mean that one person keeps solving the problems while the other avoids responsibility. Saturn, as ruler of your fifth house of love, is in your eighth house. This can bring a more serious attitude toward relationships and a need for security, but also a re-examination of trust.

For single Virgos the first interesting period opens from 3 February to 1 March, when Venus moves through Capricorn, your fifth house. This can be a good time for new acquaintances, falling in love, socializing and emotional pleasures.

Since Mercury turns retrograde on 9 February and Mars is still retrograde, some Virgos may meet someone from the past again. Renewed contact, a conversation that was left unfinished or a re-examination of a relationship that never found a final resolution is possible.

Sometimes certain relationships become current again only so that we can understand more clearly what we want today. One of the most favorable periods for partnerships opens from 26 March to 20 April, when Venus moves through Pisces, your seventh house. Venus is exalted in Pisces, so astrology connects this position with romance, tenderness, emotional closeness and greater readiness to understand. For single Virgos this can be an interesting period for entering a relationship.

For those in a relationship, a chance to improve communication, reconcile or make joint decisions may open up. It is important not to idealize the other person.

Neptune in your eighth house can heighten expectations, so it is useful to tell apart real emotional connection from what you would like a certain relationship to become.

From 20 April to 14 May Venus moves through your eighth house. The themes of intimacy, trust and shared finances are then further emphasized.

Some Virgos could talk with their partner about living together, investments or important financial decisions. The second half of the year brings a different energy.

From 20 August to 14 September Venus moves through your sign, which can contribute to greater self-confidence, a need to give yourself more attention and a different attitude toward your own attractiveness.

Although Venus in Virgo is in the sign of its fall, this transit does not have to be unfavorable. It can encourage a need for simplicity, honesty and a more realistic view of love.

You may realize that you no longer want relationships in which you constantly analyze other people's actions and try to find an excuse for someone's behavior.

From 25 November to 19 December Venus moves through your fifth house, and from 25 November Mars also activates this area. This can be one of the more interesting periods for love, falling in love and emotional encounters.

Single Virgos could meet someone who attracts them with their seriousness, stability or the way they think about the future.

For those in a relationship, the end of the year can bring a need to devote more time to their partner, shared pleasures and plans.

## Family, home and property

Family and living space are among the more important themes of 2027. Jupiter, as ruler of your fourth house, is in Leo, your twelfth house, at the start of the year.

This symbolism can point to a need to settle certain family questions away from the public eye, or to give more attention to private life. Some Virgos could think about moving, buying property, changing their living space or settling earlier family obligations.

Since Jupiter is retrograde until 13 April, certain questions you postponed earlier may reopen in the first months of the year. You may consider several options before making a final decision.

From 7 January to 3 February Venus moves through your fourth house, bringing more favorable symbolism for family gatherings, decorating the home and creating a more pleasant living space.

An especially important change comes on 26 July, when Jupiter enters your sign. Since Jupiter rules your fourth house, its entry into your first house can link personal decisions with family circumstances.

For some Virgos this may be a period in which they decide to change where they live, become independent or start an important family project. Some could think about buying property or expanding their existing living space. For others a chance to start a private business or work from a family setting may arise.

From 16 October to 25 November Mars moves through your fourth house. This period can bring renovation, building work, investing in the house or settling practical housing questions. Mars can also heighten tension with the people you live with.

You may have different opinions about money, organizing the home or certain family decisions. From 1 to 25 November Venus further activates your fourth house, which can help certain disagreements be overcome more easily.

For many Virgos the second half of the year can mark the beginning of more significant changes in private life. Education, long journeys and contacts abroad can be important in 2027, especially for Virgos thinking about further professional training.

Venus, as ruler of your ninth house, links this area with finances, business opportunities and personal values. From 14 May to 8 June Venus moves through Taurus, your ninth house.

This is one of the more favorable periods for travel, education, passing exams, international contacts and working with people from other countries. Some Virgos could decide to enroll in a course, continue their education or gain additional knowledge that will help their professional growth.

For others this may be a period of planning a long journey or making contacts with people who live abroad. It is especially interesting that Venus then moves into your tenth house of career. This means the knowledge and contacts you develop in May and early June can also have a certain significance for your professional growth.

During Mercury retrograde from 10 June to 4 July it is important to double-check documents, bookings, contracts and deadlines. You do not have to postpone all plans, but it is useful to avoid unnecessary haste.

During the year Uranus in your tenth house can also open opportunities for professional collaboration abroad, working online or involvement in projects that call for a different way of communicating.

For some Virgos 2027 may be a year in which new knowledge becomes an important part of their professional future.

In the first half of 2027 you may sometimes feel a need to withdraw from social activities.

Until the end of July Jupiter in your twelfth house can encourage a need for peace, rest and a re-examination of relationships with people. You may realize that certain friendships no longer give you the same sense of closeness. Others may feel a need to cut down on social obligations and devote more time to themselves. This does not have to mean distancing yourself from people. Sometimes we need to make room for relationships that truly suit us. From 3 to 27 July Venus moves through your eleventh house.

This can be a favorable period for socializing, renewing friendships, shared activities and achieving plans with the support of other people. New acquaintances are possible, especially through work, social media or shared interests. Some Virgos could get a useful recommendation or make a contact that will later matter for their professional plans.

In the second half of the year, with Jupiter in your sign, the need for a more active social life may also grow. You may become more open to new acquaintances, travel or activities you postponed earlier. Do not feel obliged to be available to everyone. It is important to direct your time and energy toward relationships with mutual respect.

## Health and daily habits

Health and daily habits deserve special attention in 2027.

At the start of the year Mars is already in your first house, and on 10 January it turns retrograde. This transit can symbolize heightened activity, impatience and the need to finish a lot in a short time. That is exactly when it is important not to overdo physical exertion.

From 21 February to mid-May Mars moves through your twelfth house. This period can be connected with a need for rest, withdrawal and better organization of your daily rhythm. Some Virgos could feel that they need more time to recover from hard work.

That is why in the first months of the year it is useful not to neglect sleep, diet, regular physical activity and preventive checkups.

Throughout the year Pluto stays in Aquarius, your sixth house. This long transit can symbolize deep changes in the way you work, organize daily life and relate to your own habits.

You may realize that a certain way of functioning is no longer sustainable. You may decide to change your working hours, your diet or your attitude toward obligations.

Uranus, as ruler of your sixth house, is in your tenth house, so questions of daily rhythm may be linked with professional changes. It is especially important not to let work obligations completely determine the way you live.

From mid-May, when Mars enters your sign again, the need for physical activity and personal initiative may grow.

With Jupiter entering Virgo on 26 July, optimism and motivation to adopt healthier habits may grow. Jupiter in the first house can also encourage a bigger appetite or a tendency to overdo things, so moderation is important.
