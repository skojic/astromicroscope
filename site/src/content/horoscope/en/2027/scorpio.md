*For Scorpio by Sun sign and rising sign*

For Scorpio, 2027 will be a period in which several important areas of life are set in motion at the same time. Professional life, the work environment, family circumstances and financial security are in the foreground, along with the need to change the way things have worked so far.

This is not a year in which everything will go according to a plan made in advance. Certain circumstances may call for adapting, changing direction and re-examining decisions that once seemed quite certain.

Scorpio is a sign naturally connected with transformation, endings and new beginnings. In 2027 the process of change will not concern only the inner experience of life, but also very concrete circumstances.

One question will be especially emphasized: What needs to change for life to become more stable, better and more in tune with real needs?

The answer will not be the same for every Scorpio. For some the biggest changes will come through work, for others through family or where they live, while some people born under this sign will re-examine financial decisions, relationships or the way they divide their time and energy.

## Work and career

One of the most important themes of 2027 concerns professional life.

Mars, ruler of Scorpio, begins the year in Virgo, that is, in the eleventh house. Its retrograde in the first months of the year brings attention back to questions of professional ambition, the work environment, groups, colleagues and long-term goals.

Mars is also ruler of the sixth house, which relates to daily work, obligations, working conditions and health. That is exactly why its retrograde can be especially significant for Scorpios who are unhappy with their current job or feel that their professional circumstances have changed.

A re-examination of the way they have worked so far, relationships with colleagues, the division of responsibilities or their position within an organization is possible.

For some Scorpios this may raise the question of changing jobs, returning to earlier business negotiations or continuing a project that had been postponed.

The first months of the year are not necessarily a time for sudden decisions.

Mars retrograde points above all to the need to see what no longer works and why certain circumstances have become exhausting.

An especially important period comes when Mars, moving retrograde, returns to Leo, that is, the tenth house of career and social standing. Questions of professional position are then further emphasized.

Conversations with superiors, questions of advancement, a change of job or responsibilities that were earlier left unresolved may reopen. For some Scorpios this may be a period in which it becomes clear that their professional direction so far no longer matches their ambitions.

It is important to tell apart passing dissatisfaction from a real need for change. What is re-examined in the first months of the year can begin to take a more concrete shape from spring.

When Mars turns direct, room opens for dealing with work questions more actively. Mars returning to Virgo in May further emphasizes working with other people, teamwork, business contacts and reaching long-term goals.

Scorpios who work in larger systems, organizations or teams may face changes in the structure of the group. Some collaborations may end, while at the same time opportunities may open to connect with people who have similar professional interests.

It is important to understand that this year success will depend not only on personal effort, but also on the ability to choose the right colleagues.

In the first part of the year Jupiter stays in Leo, the tenth house of Scorpio.

This position can bring a need for professional advancement, greater visibility and recognition for work done so far. Jupiter is retrograde until 13 April, so in the first months of the year earlier expectations may come up for review.

Someone may think about changing profession, someone about advancement, and someone about whether their current job truly gives them financial and personal security. Jupiter here can point to opportunities, but that does not mean every offer will automatically be favorable.

It is important to assess the real conditions, the long-term obligations and the financial sense of business decisions. From the second half of April professional plans may become clearer, while the end of July brings a change in Jupiter's position and a different focus.

Moving into Virgo, the eleventh house, Jupiter emphasizes groups, friendships, professional networks and long-term goals. For some Scorpios working with other people will become an important factor in professional growth. Financial questions are another important theme of the year.

Jupiter, ruler of the second house of personal income and material security, stays in the tenth house in the first part of the year. This links the financial situation with career, professional status and business decisions. Income may depend on changes at work, new responsibilities, advancement or a different way of doing business. Jupiter's retrograde until April points to the need to consider financial plans carefully.

Exaggerated expectations and investments that have not been checked well enough should especially be avoided. This does not mean every investment will be unfavorable, only that it is important to see the real possibilities and risks.

Uranus in Gemini, the eighth house, brings extra dynamism to finances. The eighth house relates to shared property, loans, debts, inheritance, a partner's income and money we manage together with other people.

Uranus in this area can symbolize changeable circumstances and the need for more flexibility.

Some Scorpios may face unplanned expenses or changes in financial agreements. On the other hand, different ways of financing, new business models or opportunities not considered before may also appear.

It is especially important that financial relationships with other people are clearly defined.

When it comes to loans, borrowing, joint investments or dividing property, one should not rely only on verbal agreements.

From the end of July, as Jupiter moves into the eleventh house, financial opportunities may be more connected with business contacts, group projects and long-term plans.

In the autumn, when Mars moves through Sagittarius, the second house, financial questions come to the fore again. This may be a period of more activity around earnings, but also of higher expenses.

That is why throughout the year it will be useful to tell apart what is desirable at the moment from what is sustainable in the long run.

Pluto, ruler of Scorpio, stays in Aquarius, the fourth house, all year. This is one of the most significant long-term influences for people born under this sign. The fourth house represents home, family, parents, property, origins and an inner sense of security. Pluto in this area points to a process of deep re-examination of family circumstances and the foundations of life.

For some Scorpios the question of moving, buying or selling property, renovating or organizing family life differently may come up. For others the changes will be more connected with relationships among family members.

Certain family themes that were suppressed for a long time may now call for a more serious approach. Pluto does not speak of superficial changes. Its symbolism means facing what can no longer stay the same.

That is why some Scorpios may feel a need to become independent, set clearer boundaries or take on a different role within the family. It is important to stress that this process lasts for years, so 2027 is only one of its stages.

The connection between the fourth and eighth houses is especially interesting, since Uranus, as the modern ruler of Aquarius, is in Gemini.

That is why questions of home and property may be linked with shared finances, loans, inheritance or property relations.

For Scorpios planning to buy or sell property, it will be important to consider all the financial and legal circumstances carefully.

## Love and partnership

Venus, ruler of the seventh house of partnership and marriage, moves through different areas of life during the year and so opens several periods that matter for relationships. Scorpios begin the year with Venus in their sign. This can bring a greater need for closeness, attention, emotional connection and personal fulfillment.

For single Scorpios the start of the year may be a period in which they give more attention to their own wishes and the way they approach relationships. In February, when Venus moves through the third house, communication gains special importance. Renewed contacts, more conversations or acquaintances through everyday surroundings are possible.

March further emphasizes family circumstances, but the end of the month brings a markedly different atmosphere. When Venus moves into Pisces, the fifth house, at the end of March, one of the more pleasant periods of the year for emotional life opens up.

Venus is exalted in Pisces, which emphasizes the need for tenderness, romance, understanding and emotional fulfillment. For single Scorpios this period can bring new acquaintances or greater openness to love. For those in a relationship, the need to refresh the relationship and devote more time to shared pleasures is emphasized.

The fifth house is also connected with children, creativity and personal interests, so this period may be significant for family joys or creative activities as well. An especially important period for love and marriage comes in mid-May, when Venus moves into Taurus, the seventh house. Venus is in its domicile in Taurus, so its symbolism is strongly expressed here.

This can be a favorable period for improving existing relationships, conversations about a shared future, reconciliation or important partnership decisions. For some Scorpios the question of living together or making a relationship official may come up. The quality of this period also depends on the real circumstances and the state of the existing relationship.

Venus can make connecting easier, but it cannot on its own solve problems that call for an honest conversation and shared responsibility. In June, as Venus moves into the eighth house, trust, intimacy and shared finances gain more importance. Questions of material security and emotional closeness may be more strongly linked. In September, when Venus moves through the twelfth house, some Scorpios may feel a need to withdraw and see their feelings more clearly.

This position can symbolize more discreet relationships, unspoken emotions or a need for emotional peace. It does not have to bring a secret relationship; for many it will simply be about a need for privacy. From October, when Venus moves through Scorpio again, attention returns to personal wishes, self-confidence and the relationship with oneself.

This may be a period in which people born under this sign know more clearly what they want from a relationship.

## Health and daily habits

Health and daily rhythm are among the more important themes of 2027. Saturn and Neptune stay in Aries, the sixth house of Scorpio. Saturn in this area emphasizes responsibility toward one's own body, work habits and daily obligations.

Many Scorpios may become more aware of how much work, stress and lack of rest affect their quality of life. This is a year in which it will be important to set a clearer schedule, divide energy more sensibly and avoid prolonged exhaustion. Neptune in the sixth house can symbolically point to the need to listen more carefully to the body's signals and not to ignore recurring complaints.

Mars, ruler of the sixth house, deserves special attention. In the first months of the year Mars is retrograde, and it spends part of that period in Virgo.

In traditional astrological symbolism Virgo is connected with the intestines, digestion and the digestive system. That is why an astrological reading can single out the theme of a proper diet, regular meals, digestion and the effect of daily rhythm on the body's general condition.

This year it is especially useful for Scorpios not to wait for the moment of complete exhaustion before changing their habits. The most important message of this part of the year is that responsibility toward work must not come at the expense of health. The eleventh house will be active for a significant part of the year.

Mars in Virgo at the start of the year, and then its return to this sign in mid-May, emphasize friendships, groups, social contacts and long-term goals. The attitude toward certain people may change.

Some friendships may grow stronger, while others will show that the same closeness or shared interest no longer exists. In a business sense, this is an area that can bring important collaborations.

Mars retrograde in the first months points to the need to re-examine existing agreements. Not every collaboration will have the same potential, nor will every joint project be worth the time invested. From the end of July, when Jupiter moves into Virgo, the eleventh house gets extra support.

This period can open room for new acquaintances, widening one's professional network and reaching goals that call for working with other people. For Scorpios who work with an audience, clients or within larger communities, the second part of the year can be especially significant. Mercury rules the eighth and eleventh houses of Scorpio.

That is why its retrograde periods may especially emphasize shared finances, loans, agreements, friendships, collaborations and long-term plans.

In February and early March questions that were earlier left unresolved may reopen. In the middle of the year financial agreements or plans that involve other people may come up for review. In October attention should again turn to communication, paperwork and aligning expectations.

Mercury retrograde does not mean all activities have to stop.

These periods can be useful for reviewing existing contracts, correcting mistakes and returning to projects that need extra attention. Most important of all is not to draw conclusions from incomplete information.

In summer Mars moves through Libra, the twelfth house. This period can bring a need for withdrawal, rest or finishing work done away from the public eye.

Some Scorpios may feel tired after the intense professional events of the first half of the year. That is why this part of the summer does not have to be a time for pushing harder. Finishing obligations already started and preparing for the next period can be much more useful.

At the beginning of September Mars enters Scorpio, its own sign. This marks an important change of energy.

After months of re-examining, adapting and dealing with professional questions, people born under this sign may feel a stronger need to take the initiative again.

Mars in the first house symbolically emphasizes determination, activity, personal goals and the need to get something moving. For some Scorpios this may be a period of important personal decisions. Starting new projects, changing one's way of life or setting clearer boundaries is possible. The heightened energy can also bring impatience.

That is why it is important that decisions are not the result of momentary anger or the need to solve everything at once. This period can be very useful when there is a clear goal and a well-thought-out plan.

In mid-October Mars moves into Sagittarius, the second house. Financial questions become active again. Greater motivation to earn, starting additional work or settling material obligations is possible. At the same time, expenses may rise.

It is especially important not to enter financial decisions out of momentary enthusiasm alone. The end of November brings Mars into Capricorn, the third house.

Communication, paperwork, short trips, negotiations and everyday travel are then emphasized. This can be an active period in which several practical questions will need to be settled.

It is important to avoid haste, especially in traffic and when making decisions that call for concentration.

## Closing message for Scorpio

2027 will ask Scorpios to be ready to recognize the difference between what they want to keep and what really needs to change.

Professional life may go through a significant reorganization, family circumstances may call for a different approach, and financial security will become an important criterion when making decisions.

The essence of this year is not only in outer changes.

It is just as important to change one's attitude toward one's own time, obligations, health and the people with whom one shares life plans.

The first half of the year is more focused on re-examining and dealing with existing circumstances, while the second half can bring more room for new goals and personal initiative.

Scorpios who are ready to look carefully at their situation, without needing to control or speed up everything, can come out of this year with a much clearer idea of which path they want to follow.

The most important changes of 2027 will concern not only what Scorpios do, but also the way they want to live.
