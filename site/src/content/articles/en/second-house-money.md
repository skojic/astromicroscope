In every chart there are several channels and ways of earning money. It is important to recognize the primary channel, because it shows how a person most naturally achieves material security.

These channels are most visible through the second house, the planets in it, or those in contact with the second house. The most decisive factor in interpretation is always the one that is most emphasized and in high dignity.

The second house is the house of matter: it speaks of everything we own, of our talents, abilities, skills and material values. It represents all movable property, but also the inner sense of worth.
Assessing the second house means analyzing the sign in which it begins and continues, the planets placed in it, and the ruler of the second house, its position, aspects and relationship with other houses. That ruler shows where and how money comes.

The main significator of money is Jupiter, while Venus, as the natural ruler of the second house, speaks more of material goods, enjoyment and the sense of worth than of money itself. People who inherit larger sums often have Venus or Jupiter connected with the second house, because for them money comes easily, without major obstacles.

If the ruler of the Ascendant is exalted, the person often believes that everything will go smoothly and that things will sort themselves out. Still, such a placement can bring hasty decisions and reliance on luck. When the energy of that ruler is directed the right way, it describes a person who quickly recognizes the right opportunities and can succeed with little effort.

The sign of Taurus is especially important because it is the natural ruler of the second house. It describes our financial situation, but also our value system: what we truly value and how much we believe in our ability to attract abundance.

Besides the second, the eighth house plays a strong role, because it speaks of money that comes from others: partners, inheritance, investments or shared resources.
For a person to be truly financially stable and open to abundance, there needs to be a good connection between the second and the eighth house, because money flows where there is exchange, just as life asks us to receive, but also to give.
