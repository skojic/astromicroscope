Choosing a university is one of the most important steps in a young person's life, because it shapes their future in a significant way. Parents often play a key role in this process, trying to steer their children toward the choices they consider best for them. Their influence, however, can sometimes lead young people to choose their studies not by their own interests and talents but by the wishes of the family. This is especially true when parents push their children toward "safe" or "traditional" professions. Such an approach can leave young people feeling obliged, which makes it harder for them to decide about their future on their own.

Unfortunately, the consequences of such decisions only become clear later, often toward the end of their studies, when young people realize they are not happy with the path they chose. This moment of facing reality often brings regret and the feeling that the choice was driven by the wrong motives: parental pressure, social expectations, or not knowing their own interests and potential well enough.

## Astrology as support in choosing an educational and career path

Astrology can be seen as a tool for better understanding personal potential and interests, and in that way it can give young people real support in making such important decisions. My own experience in this area has shown me how much astrology can help, not only young people but parents too, to recognize true potential and guide their children onto a path that matches who they really are. As a parent, I tried not to impose my own expectations or family traditions that could limit my children's choices. Instead, I focused on encouraging them to choose an educational path that inspires and motivates them.

## Astrological indicators for choosing studies and a career

The ninth house of the natal chart is connected with higher education, with starting and finishing one's studies, while the tenth house symbolizes a professional career and one's position in society. We can say that the ninth house paves the way to the tenth, because a good choice of education often leads to a successful career.

Mercury, the planet that rules intellect and learning, plays a key role when we look at potential for education. It shows how quickly we learn, how we apply what we have learned and which fields come more easily to us. By analyzing Mercury in the natal chart, we can identify the fields a young person naturally prefers, as well as the learning styles that suit them best.

Based on these astrological indicators, young people can gain a clearer view of their abilities and inclinations, which can make the choice of university and career easier. In this way astrology can be a powerful tool for becoming aware of one's own potential and making decisions that lead to lasting satisfaction and success. At any level of education, Mercury plays a big role because it speaks of practical intellect. It tells us how quickly we learn, whether we can apply what we have learned, which fields come more easily, what we remember better and what truly interests us.

Mercury, the planet of communication and intellect, reflects specific ways of thinking, learning and educational inclinations in each sign of the zodiac. Its position shows how a person thinks and the fields in which they can realize their intellectual potential.

## Sun, Moon and Ascendant

When analyzing studies, it is important to take into account the key factors of the natal chart: the Sun, the Moon and the Ascendant.

### Sun

The Sun symbolizes character, temperament and the rational part of consciousness, showing how we express ourselves in the outer world. When the Sun is strong (in a favorable sign), it gives creativity, energy and the ability to shape one's life independently. A weak Sun suggests being more subject to fate. Its position and aspects to the 9th and 10th houses point to professional inclinations and the nature of one's studies.

### Steiner's typology of temperaments

Rudolf Steiner (1861–1925) was an Austrian philosopher, scientist, educator, writer and spiritual teacher, known as the founder of anthroposophy, a spiritual movement that brings spiritual insight together with science and art.

Rudolf Steiner divided temperament into four basic types, which are linked to the elements of the horoscope:

- **Sanguine** (air signs: Gemini, Libra, Aquarius): Sociable, optimistic and communicative, quickly loses interest in monotony. Drawn to creative and team activities.

- **Choleric** (fire signs: Aries, Leo, Sagittarius): An energetic, decisive leader who likes a challenge. Suited to dynamic, productive tasks.

- **Phlegmatic** (water signs: Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces): Calm, patient and systematic, enjoys routine and activities that call for consistency.

- **Melancholic** (earth signs: Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn): Introspective and sensitive, drawn to art and analytical tasks.

### Moon

The Moon symbolizes emotions and inner motivation, and by its sign it points to professions:

- **Moon in Aries**: Dynamic occupations: sport, the military, creative and competitive fields.

- **Moon in Taurus**: Stable professions: economics, agriculture, ecology, architecture.

- **Moon in Gemini**: Communication and research: journalism, marketing, education.

- **Moon in Cancer**: Caring professions: psychology, medicine, social work.

- **Moon in Leo**: Creative fields: art, leadership, the performing arts.

- **Moon in Virgo**: Analytical, precise fields: medicine, science, administration.

- **Moon in Libra**: Diplomatic and aesthetic professions: law, art.

- **Moon in Scorpio**: Research fields: psychotherapy, criminology, science.

- **Moon in Sagittarius**: Travel and philosophy: tourism, international relations, teaching.

- **Moon in Capricorn**: Stable, structured professions: management, engineering, the military.

- **Moon in Aquarius**: Innovative fields: IT, technology, research.

- **Moon in Pisces**: Creativity and art: music, writing, film, psychology.

**The Sun and the Moon** together shape motivation, while **the Ascendant** shows personal expression and the approach to choosing studies and a career. The right combination of these factors can contribute a great deal to choosing a suitable education and professional path.

## Example 1

Before analyzing the ninth house, which describes the field of study, I focused on the person's aptitude for learning and on Mercury's interests.

**An Aquarius Ascendant** points to an original, unconventional person who constantly strives to improve. Neptune on the Ascendant highlights artistic inclinations, creativity and talent. The ruler of the Ascendant, Uranus in Aquarius in the first house, emphasizes a focus on personal development. Uranus sextile the Sun, ruler of the seventh house, shows an orientation toward the public and recognition, and also the ability to stand out both as an individual and as part of a group.

**Mercury in Pisces**, the sign of its fall, but on the degree of Venus's exaltation, brings deep, abstract and visionary thoughts. The third house, in Taurus, points to a need for security and stability. Jupiter in the third house speaks of broad interests and a drive for knowledge, while Mars adds energy and dynamism to communication and learning. The presence of Saturn brings seriousness and responsibility.

The ruler of the third house, Venus in the second house, connects knowledge with acquiring personal and material values. Its conjunction with Mercury points to focused intelligence, productivity and organization.

**The Sun in Aries**, its sign of exaltation, gives the person creativity, courage and energy. As a fire sign, Aries is linked to the choleric temperament, a strong will and determination. This position is ideal for technical studies and engineering work.

### The ninth house and education

The ninth house, in Scorpio on the degree of the Moon's fall, points to something out of the ordinary at the start of studies. The decision to take a gap year is confirmed by the position of Mars (ruler of the ninth house) in the third house conjunct Saturn, which points to delay: Saturn.

**Mars, in Taurus between Jupiter (space) and Saturn (time),** leads to the decision to study architecture. The Moon in Cancer, sextile Mars and Saturn, supports this decision because it symbolizes building, stability and permanence. The Moon in the sixth house, sextile Jupiter (ruler of the eleventh house), points to work connected with groups and associations, especially around housing and real estate.

### The tenth house and career

The tenth house, which begins in Scorpio, shows that the person will work in the field they studied, while the house continuing into Sagittarius and Jupiter in the third house suggest long-term progress. Mars conjunct Saturn in the third house emphasizes creating something lasting and significant. By dispositorship Mars leads to Aries, the sign of engineering and building, trine Pluto (ruler of the ninth and tenth houses), which further confirms professional success in the field she trained for.

In this example too, Mars as ruler of the ninth house is trine Venus, the dispositor of the Moon, which is one more indicator of studying architecture.

## Example 4

**A Capricorn Ascendant** points to a person who is ambitious, disciplined and responsible. These qualities are key to success in demanding, structured fields such as law. Capricorn brings perseverance and a focus on long-term goals, which matters for studies that call for dedication and a detailed understanding of legal norms.

**The Sun in Libra** belongs to the sanguine temperament, which gives the person sociability, communication skills and curiosity. As the seventh sign of the zodiac, Libra is linked with justice and a strong need to create balance and harmony, both in personal relationships and in a professional setting. This position points to a strong sense of justice and fairness, a key quality for the legal profession.

**The Moon in Virgo** contributes analytical skill, attention to detail and a readiness to be of service, all necessary for solving legal questions and advising clients. Its energy further emphasizes the ability to understand and work with people, as well as the willingness to commit to solving complex problems.

**Mercury**, as the symbol of intellect and communication, placed in Libra, supports an understanding of legal subjects and the skill of argument. Its position in the seventh sign of the zodiac emphasizes an interest in public relations, mediation and establishing legal and social balance.

University is symbolically represented by the ninth house, which in this case is in Libra. This position clearly points to legal studies, because the ninth house traditionally rules higher education and justice, while Libra further strengthens the interest in law and justice in the broader sense.

Venus, as the ruler of Libra, placed in Scorpio, forms a harmonious sextile with the Moon, ruler of the seventh house, which further emphasizes the themes of justice, the public and legal work. This astrological combination suggests that law school would be an excellent choice, giving the person the chance to use her natural abilities for analysis, negotiation and establishing legal and social balance. Venus, as ruler of the ninth house, is also supported by Saturn, ruler of the Ascendant, so this person finished her studies on time.

## Conclusion

Knowing astrology can do a lot to broaden young people's horizons as they make decisions about education and career. Understanding astrological influences, such as planetary positions and an analysis of the ninth and tenth houses in the natal chart, allows young people to recognize their own potential, talents and inclinations. Astrology can give them deeper insight into their abilities and interests, which makes it easier to choose a path that is true to who they are.

Used as a tool for self-knowledge, astrological analysis can help young people see the bigger picture of their possibilities, recognize their inner resources and align them with outer circumstances and the demands of society. This approach can reduce the pressure of social expectations and parental projections, allowing them to make independent, informed decisions about their future.

At a time when the choice of educational path is key to personal and professional development, astrology can be a valuable ally, helping young people discover their unique qualities and step confidently toward their life goals. In this way it not only supports their individual development but also contributes to society through empowered, self-aware individuals.
