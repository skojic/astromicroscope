On 30 June 2026 Jupiter enters Leo and stays there until 26 July 2027. The last time this benefic planet was in this sign was from 16 July 2014 to 11 August 2015, and back then it set off many important events in Leo's symbolism: successes in sport, culture, public life and big projects. In that period the World Trade Center in New York opened, Real Madrid won the FIFA Club World Cup in Marrakesh, and the prime ministers of Serbia and China opened the Pupin Bridge in Belgrade. It was a time of great achievements, bold moves and the need to leave a mark.

Jupiter rules Sagittarius and the ninth house, which stands for growth, development, knowledge, travel and the expansion of consciousness. Leo is the fifth sign of the zodiac and speaks of everything we do out of love, pleasure and a sincere wish of the heart. The fifth house is connected with children, creativity, sport, the stage, falling in love, enjoyment, authenticity and the joy of living.

Every twelve years Jupiter comes back to the same sign, and it spends roughly a year in each one. It is the planet of growth and expansion, so it magnifies everything it touches: for one person work and success, for another money, and for someone else even health challenges, if it is poorly aspected in the natal chart. That is why its position and aspects matter so much.

While it moves through Leo, Jupiter will spread the energy of the heart, creativity and authentic expression. It will awaken the need to shine as we truly are, without hiding or adapting to others. It brings more courage to those who want to be seen, to express themselves through what they love and to work out of love.

It will give special support to people who work with children, to athletes, actors, artists, teachers and everyone who is in the public eye. This is a period in which many can develop healthy self-confidence and a stronger sense of their own worth.

Jupiter in Leo will bring changes in education too. Young people will be motivated to express their wishes more clearly, to learn to stand up in front of others and show who they really are. Those who find the courage to step onto life's stage and take responsibility for themselves will be rewarded.

From 30 June it is no longer the time to follow others blindly. This is a period in which we learn to follow ourselves, our heart and our dreams. Jupiter will remind us to live our Sun, because the Sun is exactly what it activates over the coming year. If we have been asleep, passive or insecure, the time of awakening is here.

It is time to ask ourselves:

- What do we love?
- What makes us happy?
- Who or what warms our Sun?

The answer is often not in others but in ourselves. We are the main creators of our destiny. The Sun was not made to hide in the dark but to shine. It recognizes love, joy, beauty, warmth and optimism. So get ready to spread positive energy, because everything you send out into the world comes back to you.

## Jupiter in Leo for each sign of the zodiac

### Aries

For Aries, Jupiter brings a lot of energy, lucky circumstances, more fun, travel and falling in love. Many will want to spend the summer at faraway destinations, and some may meet a kindred spirit on exactly such a trip. Those who work in sport, art or creative fields can achieve great success. Young Aries starting secondary school may choose creative tracks, marketing, acting, sport or tourism. A very lucky year for the first sign of the zodiac.

### Taurus

For Taurus, Jupiter moves through the fourth house and brings expansion around family and home. Many will want to grow their family, move to a bigger place or even start a life abroad. This is a very favorable period for investing in property, solving the housing question and starting a private business.

### Gemini

For Gemini, Jupiter moves through the third house and brings new knowledge, travel, acquaintances and wider horizons. This transit brings more optimism and ease, but also a tendency to overdo things if Jupiter is poorly aspected. The year is excellent for learning, signing contracts, communication, short trips and buying a car.

### Cancer

For Cancer, Jupiter brings an expansion of personal and material values. Many will grow in self-confidence, and in finances too. This transit can bring higher earnings through creativity, management skills or work with partners abroad. The period brings lucky circumstances and personal progress.

### Leo

For Leo, Jupiter moves through the first house and puts them at the center of attention. It brings personal growth, success, new opportunities and a feeling of prosperity. Since the first house describes the body, some Leos may also gain weight, especially if Jupiter is challengingly aspected. Still, for most this is a year of great growth and standing out.

### Virgo

For Virgo, Jupiter moves through the twelfth house and brings a deep inner transformation. This is a period when they can quietly discover their true purpose and realize how strong they really are. Solitude can become healing. Many Virgos will want to travel far, spend time by the sea or devote themselves to art, research and work behind the scenes.

### Libra

After twelve years, Libra gets the chance to stand out in society again and show its authenticity. Many will achieve significant results through projects, social activities and new acquaintances. This is a period of widening contacts and reaching important goals.

### Scorpio

For Scorpio, Jupiter brings many benefits in career and work. Many will get a promotion, more responsibility or the chance to work with partners abroad. This is a period of professional growth, especially if Jupiter is well placed in the natal chart.

### Sagittarius

For Sagittarius, Jupiter moves through the natural ninth house and brings a need for learning, travel and personal growth. Many will finally begin to live their purpose. Students can be very lucky, as can those dealing with legal matters or planning to move abroad.

### Capricorn

For Capricorn, Jupiter moves through the eighth house and brings support from other people, partners or family abroad. Some may come into larger sums of money through partnerships, an inheritance or joint investments. The year is favorable for investing and financial changes.

### Aquarius

For Aquarius, Jupiter moves through the seventh house and brings important partnerships. Single Aquarians may enter a serious relationship, while those already in one may take it to a new level. Business partnerships, legal matters and work with partners abroad can also bring success.

### Pisces

For Pisces, Jupiter moves through the sixth house and brings the chance to stand out through work and daily activities. Many Pisces may get a promotion, expand their business or start working with partners abroad. Creativity and art will be especially emphasized, and luck will follow them all year.

As you can see, I have described how Jupiter can affect each sign of the zodiac, but it is very important to read for your rising sign as well. Although Jupiter is the planet of luck, prosperity and growth, it can also show its other side. Sometimes it can lead us to think we can do anything, and after overdoing it we end up feeling exhausted and empty.

That is why it is important to look at Jupiter's position in the natal chart and its aspects, to best understand its influence during the transit through Leo.

You surely know the saying "easy come, easy go."
That is exactly Jupiter's symbolism when we do not know our limits: it gives, expands and multiplies, but it can just as easily take away if we do not know how to handle its energy.
