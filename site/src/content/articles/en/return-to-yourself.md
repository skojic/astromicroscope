**The return to yourself begins the moment you stop waiting to be rescued from outside and decide to set your soul free.**

## A burden we did not choose

In my work with clients I often meet heavy themes: the anxieties a soul carries, the stories imposed by families and ancestors.
Some people soak it all up like a sponge: every emotion, every pain, the sorrow of a father, a mother, of loved ones… and then cling to them even more tightly.

I hear how hard it is for them. How they struggle to find a way out, without support, will or motivation.
Mothers who could not find a solution and had to leave their home and family.
Children who need their mother are left without her, and it is precisely she who, in the first four years, is the foundation of a child's development.
And she… leaves.

A story moves me and I wonder: why don't those who have a lot of money share it with families who have none?
Then I realize that the one who has wants even more, while the one who has little often gives that little to help someone who has even less.

## It is not for us to judge

People around us constantly comment on one another:
“This one is like this, that one is like that, wrong about this or that…”
But they are all our mirrors. They reflect what we ourselves carry inside, deeply hidden and suppressed.

Who are we to judge others?
We do not know which road they have walked, how old their soul is, how much experience, pain and anxiety it carries.
We do not know their past lives, and often we do not understand even our own well enough.

We may envy the rich, but perhaps they are there precisely to remind us that we can do more and better.
Whatever our material situation, each of us has received as much as we have earned.

## Acceptance is the key

I often heard Saturn called the teacher and tormentor, the symbol of karma.
The key to healing is acceptance: of everything and everyone around you, without judging, criticizing or complaining.

No one can help you until you roll up your sleeves and start clearing space for your soul.
Our body is the home of our soul, and it must be vital, healthy and a clear channel.

If we have a problem, many memories, experiences and blocked emotions have settled inside us.
The first step is to become aware that we are responsible for our own life.

## The Sun and the Moon: our first teachers

We must not forget how we came into this world: father Sun and mother Moon.
Whatever our experience with them, however present they were or not, they are our stars and our guiding lights.

The Sun is the strongest, so strong that we cannot look at it. The Moon reflects that light.
In the same way, a father in our life must take the leading role: to give security and to build confidence and strength.

The mother, the Moon, reflects that strength and gives the child emotional security, embrace and warmth.
Today, however, the roles are often reversed: the father's presence weakens, and the mother takes on the burden of both parents.

Still, what matters most is gratitude. Because without the Sun and the Moon, we would not exist.

## Childhood memories: deep roots

What we experience in early childhood shapes our life deeply.
The Moon describes the mother, and when it is in a challenging position it often speaks of her difficult emotions in our first years.
A poorly aspected Sun can point to an absent father, which later echoes as a lack of will, strength and self-confidence.

But when we accept all these parts of ourselves and see them as parts of our soul waiting to be joined into a whole, that is when the magic happens.

## The power of forgiveness

Learning to love your life, whatever the circumstances, means diving into yourself and discovering your power.
I would not be where I am today had I not forgiven those who hurt me, above all my family of origin and those I thought would always be there, and were not. Thanks to them, today I am strong, happy and content, because I learned that I can manage on my own.

**Only when you realize that you have to do it alone do you get to know yourself, your true values and your boundaries.**

## What is yours will always find you

If you feel someone has wronged you, know this: no one can take from you what rightfully belongs to you.
If something is yours, it will come back to you when the right time comes.
If it does not come back, it was never meant to be.

Stop feeling sorry for yourself, because in that moment you become helpless, both to yourself and to others.
To regain power over your life, you have to stand on your own feet and take responsibility.
You are not here to follow others, but to leave your own authentic mark.

## The age of awakening

The time of awakening has begun. The Age of Aquarius calls us to honesty, change and freedom.
Free yourself from the patterns that hold you back and limit you.
Be yourself.
Take your life into your own hands and do not expect too much from others.

**Because coming back to yourself is the most beautiful gift you can give yourself** 🌙
