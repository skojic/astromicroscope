## Why a parent should know their child's natal chart

Many people think that reading a child's natal chart means interfering with the child's free will.
But the truth is quite the opposite.

The natal chart **does not limit the child**; it gives the parent a map for understanding, a way to see their child as the child truly is, with all their particularities, talents and inner world.

Every child carries a unique energy, a family story and a genetic code that comes not only from the parents but also from the ancestors. Sometimes a trait, inclination or talent awakens in a child that the parent cannot understand right away.
Through the natal chart a parent can discover **the secrets of the child's soul**:

- how to approach the child,
- whether the child reacts more through emotion, reason or movement,
- how much closeness and security matter to them, and how much freedom and exploration,
- in which area they can shine,
- what drives them and what puts them under pressure.

The chart can also show whether a child is more introverted or socially oriented, and whether they need sport, art, nature or intellectual activities.

A parent who knows their child's natal chart does not take away the child's freedom; on the contrary, they **give the child the freedom to be who they are**.
Instead of projecting their own expectations, they learn to understand the child's nature, to support them and guide them in a direction that is in harmony with their soul.

Because every child arrives with a story of their own.
And astrology is simply a language that helps a parent hear that story before the child is able to tell it.
