Essence

Astromicroscope looks at astrology closely and explains what it sees. The microscope stands for precision and method; the night sky for astrology itself; the light pages for clarity. Education comes first, and private analyses are the exclusive part of the offer.

Precise

Every claim is backed by the chart and explained. A research astrologer's eye.

Clear

Simple, practical language. No jargon without an explanation.

Calm

No fatalism and no fear. A chart shows tendencies and timing, not a verdict.

Elegant

Light pages, deep blue, a touch of gold. Quality without decoration.

Rules

  • Sentence case everywhere. Capitals only in YouTube thumbnail titles, which follow Gorica's existing thumbnail style.
  • Reading text at most 62 characters per line.
  • One H1 per page; headings never skip a level.
  • No italic for emphasis inside headings; italic is for quotes and the second line of a pull quote.
  • Fonts are self-hosted from brand/fonts; never load them from a third-party server.

Motion

One signature effect, the star field, and calm transitions everywhere else.

Star field (hero only)

Stars twinkle slowly; near the cursor they brighten, drift toward it and open into four-point sparkles; moving the cursor leaves a short glitter trail.

It starts after the page is ready, pauses when the hero is out of view, is simplified on phones and switches off completely when the visitor asks for reduced motion.

Everything else

Transitions of 150–250 ms with a soft ease. Things respond to the visitor (opening, expanding, confirming); nothing moves on its own, and nothing slides in while scrolling.

The chart lens follows the pointer and also moves with the arrow keys.

Photography

Real, warm and calm. Gorica teaching, working with charts, or in a simple portrait.

Use

  • Soft daylight; white, beige or blue walls.
  • Gorica at work: printed charts, notes, a laptop, a whiteboard.
  • Calm colours that sit with the palette.
  • The arch crop for portraits.

Avoid

  • Mystical stock clichés: crystal balls, smoke, purple nebulae, tarot props.
  • Heavy filters and strong colour casts.
  • Busy backgrounds behind the logo.
  • Text baked into photos on the website (it cannot be read by search engines or screen readers).

Social media

Voice

Gorica speaks like a good teacher: clear, warm and exact. She addresses the reader informally and explains how she reached each conclusion.

PrincipleSayAvoid
Answer first“The eighth house describes shared resources and deep change. Here is how to read it.”“The mysterious eighth house hides many secrets…”
Show the method“Your Moon is in the eighth house, square Saturn. That is why…”“The stars say that…”
No fatalism“This period asks for patience.”“This transit will ruin your relationships.”
Precise dates“Saturn in Aries: 14 Feb 2026 – 13 Apr 2028.”“Soon, Saturn changes everything.”
Plain actions“Explore courses”, “Request an analysis”“Discover your destiny”, “Click here”
  • Serbian first (Latin script, ekavian), English adapted, both proofread.
  • No medical, financial or legal promises.
  • Emoji rarely, and never in headings.
