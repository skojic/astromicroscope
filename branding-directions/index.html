Astromicroscope: website directions

Website and brand directions for Gorica Vićentijević, graduated astrologer (natal, predictive and karmic astrology). Each direction is a full home page plus a brand snapshot at the bottom (logo idea, palette, type, two Instagram posts, voice). Open each one, scroll to the end, and try the interactive hero.

Pick one direction, or a mix (for example, “B's colour with A's lens”), and the full brand kit is built from that choice.

Takes the name literally. The chart is a specimen and Gorica is the trained eye. Calm, precise, credible: astrology with the manners of a laboratory.

D · Midnight Lens builds on direction A

Direction A's microscope idea with a Midnight night-sky hero. Stars across the hero twinkle, open into sparkles near the cursor and leave a short glitter trail as it moves, adapted from the dot grid on the Kvit home page. Gorica's existing G-and-microscope logo is redrawn as vector and used across the site.
