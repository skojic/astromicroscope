Astromicroscope: website directions

Four website and brand directions for Gorica Vićentijević, graduated astrologer. All four follow the brief: an education-first site (programs, recorded courses, digital products, articles), with natal, solar and karmic analyses and life-area consultations as the premium, one-to-one offer. Pages are mostly white and soft beige, with blue from light to deep and small gold details.

Each direction is a full home page with a brand snapshot at the bottom. Open each one, try the interactive parts, and scroll to the end. Pick one, or combine parts, for example “D's logo with C's colour code”.
