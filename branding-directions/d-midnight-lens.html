Gorica studied astrology at university level and holds a degree in it. She treats a chart the way a scientist treats a sample: carefully, without rushing to conclusions, and with every claim explained.

Her readings are in English and Serbian. No fatalism, no vague promises. You leave knowing what the chart says, how she got there, and what you can do with it.

Direction D · Brand snapshot

Direction A's microscope idea, now carrying Gorica's existing mark. The hero becomes a night sky in Midnight, and the lab colours (slide glass, gentian, eosin) carry the rest of the site. Gold belongs to the logo and the sparkles only. That keeps the familiar gold-on-blue avatar recognisable without turning the whole brand into the gold-and-navy look every astrology account uses.

Primary mark, redrawn as vector

The G of Gorica, with a microscope built into its counter and three stars beside it. Same idea and composition as the current Instagram avatar, now drawn as clean vector shapes, so it stays sharp from favicon to print.
