Direction C · Brand snapshot

The idea: a school, not a salon. The four elements become a colour code that runs through everything, from the logo and the sign tiles to course levels and Instagram carousels, the way a good textbook colour-codes its chapters. It is the most scalable direction for courses and printed materials. The bold move is the system: twelve tiles, four colours, one chunky condensed typeface.
