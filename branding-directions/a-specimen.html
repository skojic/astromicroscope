Direction A · Brand snapshot

The idea: take the name literally. Astromicroscope is the instrument; the chart is the specimen; Gorica is the trained eye. Astrology presented with the calm and precision of a lab, set apart from the gold-on-navy mysticism that fills the category. The one bold move is the lens: it appears in the logo, the hero, and as a crop device on every Instagram post.
