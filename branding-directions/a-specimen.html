Direction A · Brand snapshot

The name taken literally: Astromicroscope is the instrument, the chart is the sample, Gorica is the trained eye. Following the brief, this version is entirely light. White and soft beige pages, the chart drawn in blue ink, gold used only as a hairline or a single dot. Calm, precise and very simple.
