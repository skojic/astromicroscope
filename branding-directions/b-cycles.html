Direction B · Brand snapshot

The idea: build the brand on Gorica's own line, “Tvoja priroda. Tvoji ciklusi.” Astrology as rhythm and timing, dressed like a fashion house rather than a fortune teller. One saturated ultramarine owns the brand, so the feed is recognisable from across the room. The bold move is the colour itself; Bodoni carries the elegance, and everything else stays quiet.
