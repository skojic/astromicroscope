Direction B · Brand snapshot

Built on Gorica's own line, “Tvoja priroda. Tvoji ciklusi.” Astrology as rhythm and timing, styled like a fashion house. Following the brief, the pages are bone white and sand beige. Ultramarine carries the type and the actions, pale blue softens the images, and a single gold planet is the only gold.
